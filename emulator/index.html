HOW TO PLAY

  • BUTTONS, KEYS: click or touch; several fingers hold several; SHIFT+click holds one
  • SLIDE ALONG THE KEYS: each key the finger reaches plays (buttons stay held)
  • KNOBS: − / + (held: repeats, faster), drag, the wheel
  • MASTER: drag the bar; a double-click: half
  • KEYBOARD: the computer's keys play the FM-1's, B4-G5 above, F3-A4 below, then the buttons:
  • HOME + LFO (U + 0): the help for the screen shown
  • RIGHT-CLICK THE SCREEN: save or copy it
