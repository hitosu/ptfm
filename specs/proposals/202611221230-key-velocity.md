# Proposal: the keys' velocity

Status: **settled** (2026-11-22), for 1.0: sections 1-6, and section 7's MIDI CONTROLLER part; the sound's aftertouch
stays open ([../open-questions.md](../open-questions.md)). Its specs lead: [../hardware.md](../hardware.md#the-keys-velocity)
(the keys), [../ui/screens/settings.md](../ui/screens/settings.md) (KEYS VELOCITY, KEYS VOLUME, CONTROLLER AFTERTOUCH),
[../connections.md](../connections.md) and [../controller.md](../controller.md) (the velocity sent, the controller's
aftertouch), [../files/settings.md](../files/settings.md), [../debug.md](../debug.md#the-keys-bytes) (the readings),
[../panel.md](../panel.md) and [../emulator.md](../emulator.md) (the twins).

The FM-1's keys carry no velocity sensor: each is a rubber dome over one contact of the 11 x 6 diode matrix, and the
scan only asks whether it is closed. But the contact is a carbon pill whose resistance falls with the force on it, and
goes on falling while a key is pressed harder; and two of the matrix rows, PA7 and PA8, are inputs of the chip's
converter (channels 0 and 1). Those two rows hold 20 of the 27 note keys, F3 to C5. The top seven, C#5 to G5 on PA6,
have no converter. Nothing on the board changes.

## 1. Reading the keys

- **One conversion a scan tick.** After the scan latches column n it starts a conversion of that column's PA7 (even
  frames) or PA8 (odd frames) and takes the result at the next tick, 100 µs later (a conversion takes about 60 µs at
  the settings that read true). So each of the 20 keys is read every 2.2 ms. The scan's rate and its debounce stay as
  they are: the digital scan stays the truth for pressed and let go; the readings only add the velocity.
- **Driven readings.** Through the row's pull-up a seated key reads about 185 of 1023 whatever the force (the matrix
  diode's drop), and a harder press moves it 10-20. So once a key has been touched, every other reading of it is taken
  with its row driven high at the pin's second-weakest drive (HD0 alone, the level the readings' figures were measured
  at): that driver cannot hold the line against a seated pill, and the
  reading less the diode's 185 is the pill's resistance. Soft and firm presses then differ by 100-150, and firm ones
  repeat within about 5. The row is an input with its pull-up again before the next rows are read.
- **The LEDs.** A lit LED on the column being read lifts the reading by about 25: a held key's column stays dark on the
  tick that reads it (1 tick in 22 for that column).
- **MASTER and the battery** come from the scan while it reads the keys (the converter is its own then): MASTER in one
  slot a frame pair, the battery in a short pause of the readings once a second.
- **Only while KEYS VELOCITY is not CONSTANT.** At CONSTANT the scan, the converter and MASTER work exactly as before.
- **The rows' digital inputs stay on.** With them off, every key on PA7 and PA8 reads pressed, OCT− and OCT+ among
  them; a driven row left driven reads a whole row pressed. The scan's tests check the rows' direction at every read.

## 2. The velocity

- **How deep the key sits, not how fast it went down.** The dome snaps: every press lands within a reading or two
  whatever its speed, so the time between two thresholds says nothing. What tells a hard hit from a soft one is how
  low the driven reading sits just after the landing.
- **The readings of a press:** open; touched (pulled-up reading under 900: the pill has started to conduct); seated
  (under 400); open again after two readings over 600 (a landed key can read open once: a bounce). A touch that never
  seats is a graze and plays nothing.
- **The velocity** is the lowest driven reading of the first driven reading after the touch (WAIT 1), on a log scale of
  the pill's resistance between FIRM (530: velocity 127) and LIGHT (896: velocity 1), as conductive rubber's
  resistance falls as a power of the force: t = log(r / r_firm) / log(r_light / r_firm), velocity = 1 + 126 (1 − t)^c,
  c by KEYS VELOCITY (SOFT 0.47, NORMAL 0.70, HARD 1.05: softer playing reaches 127 at SOFT; CONSTANT reads nothing,
  every key at KEYS VOLUME). FM-1s differ by about
  120 at the firm end; a key pressed off its centre reads softer.
- **The note waits for it:** a key's press reaches the screens once its velocity is known, at most one reading
  (2.2 ms) after the touch, so a key's note comes about 1-2 ms later than before. A press whose velocity is not known
  10 ms after the digital scan saw it plays at KEYS VOLUME. The debug build keeps WAIT 2 (the lower of two driven
  readings, steadier, about 4.4 ms more) as a switch, to be compared on the FM-1.
- **The top row** (C#5-G5) plays at the mean of the last four sensed velocities; at KEYS VOLUME before any.

## 3. What hears it

A key's velocity is its note's volume, exactly as a MIDI note's velocity is (`01`-`7F`): the instruments hear it as
they hear a MIDI note's (a DX7's modulators too, since 1.0: harder, brighter), the EDITOR's takes and LOOPS record it, the arpeggiator
takes it, MIDI OUT's KEYS sends it as the note's velocity, MIDI CONTROLLER's notes carry it (127 and 100 before).
KEYS GAIN is unchanged. MIDI notes keep their own velocity.

## 4. SETTINGS

| item | values | default |
|---|---|---|
| KEYS VELOCITY | CONSTANT (every key at KEYS VOLUME, the scan as before), SOFT, NORMAL, HARD (the keys' actual velocity on that curve, above). Global: every song, the loops, DJ | NORMAL |
| KEYS VOLUME | the volume of the keys' notes with KEYS VELOCITY CONSTANT, and of the top row before any sensed key: `01`-`7F` | `60` |
| CONTROLLER AFTERTOUCH (the MIDI page) | what MIDI CONTROLLER's general mode sends of the keys' pressure (section 7): OFF, CHANNEL, POLY | CHANNEL |

KEYS VELOCITY and CONTROLLER AFTERTOUCH are saved with the settings (the next free field: byte 123's bits 5-4 and 7-6).

## 5. The playground, the emulator, the EDITOR page

The virtual FM-1's keys take a velocity from where they are pressed: the lower on the key, the harder (the top edge 1,
the bottom edge 127, on the curve's middle). The emulator hands it to the firmware as the FM-1's scan would; the
playground's model takes it the same way; MIRROR sends it as the key's Note On velocity. KEYS VELOCITY is in the
playground's SETTINGS and in the EDITOR page's settings as on the FM-1.

## 6. The debug build

The readings, so an FM-1's own keys can be read before the curve is trusted: the last press's key, its touch, landing
and driven readings and its velocity, the presses, grazes and timeouts counted, the scan tick's time; in STATUS and in
the figures. The checks by hand: soft, medium and hard presses on several keys, a key's centre and its edge, chords,
fast repeats, every key lit (LOOPS) and dark, MASTER turned while playing, the battery's bars, and OCT− at power-on (USB
RESCUE) with KEYS VELOCITY on.

## 7. Aftertouch

A held key's driven readings keep coming, so pressing harder than where it rests can be read as a pressure, on F3-C5
only (the top seven have no converter).

- **The sound takes it as loudness in 1.0:** a held key pressed harder makes its own notes louder, from its
  velocity's volume toward 127, heard only (specs/hardware.md); other destinations (a modulation, MIDI OUT's channel
  pressure in play) and writing it are decided on their own.
- **Recorded, decided after:** an EDITOR take and LOOPS' stage write it as volumes alone on the row after the one
  playing (a change of 4 or more, its return to the key's velocity; nothing while held steady; a row with a note left
  as it is), so the recording plays the swell; LOOPS' stage's keys are heard with it through their lanes. A full stage
  or edit memory drops the volumes, never the notes; MIDI OUT keeps the velocity. AUTOMATIONS' events stay open.
- **MIDI CONTROLLER's general mode sends it** (not mirror mode, not the tracker): CONTROLLER AFTERTOUCH CHANNEL, Channel
  Pressure (Dn) on the mode's channel, the highest of the held keys' (0 when none presses); POLY, Polyphonic Key
  Pressure (An) on each held key's note (the note its Note On sent, OCT- / OCT+'s offset with it), its 0 sent once when
  it eases to nothing and at its let-go; OFF, none. On change only; never with KEYS VELOCITY CONSTANT (nothing is read).
- **The pressure:** a per-key rest that follows the driven reading down for about 250 ms after the landing (a key goes
  on settling), then creeps after it slowly, and comes up at once when the key eases; the lean log2(rest / reading),
  0.04 of an octave nothing, 0.46 more 127; smoothed over about 30 ms, worked out every 5 ms outside the scan's tick
  (no loop or divide there). Its state packed in 16-bit fields, in the controller's own memory.
- **The twins:** a held virtual key dragged further down is pressed harder; held still, no pressure.

## 8. The cost

The scan's tick: about 1 µs more while KEYS VELOCITY is not CONSTANT (one conversion's registers, a key's state, a
driven reading's four pin writes), about 1 % of core 1; nothing in the audio. RAM: the 20 keys' state, about 150 B (the
controller's aftertouch in the controller's own memory, none of the tracker's). Not here:
a key's own calibration, the top row read through PA10 (a third frame phase, velocity only, compressed on firm
presses), the release's velocity.
