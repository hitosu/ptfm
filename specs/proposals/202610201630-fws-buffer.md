# Proposal: FWS for the firmware alone, the EDITOR's own pattern, patterns of 128 rows

Status: **settled** (2026-10-20); its specs are written (section 6), and lead. Three changes that hang together: nothing of the user's is ever kept in FWS;
the EDITOR gets a pattern of its own to start from, kept by SAVE and never listed; and a pattern has at most
16 tracks × 128 rows, so the EDITOR's own pattern always has its room.

## 1. FWS: the firmware and its factory versions

- **FWS** (0x004000-0x092000, [../files/store.md](../files/store.md#where-things-go)) holds the firmware and
  what it carries: the built-in cartridges `F0`-`FB`, FUSION's patterns `F0`-`F4`, the keys' song `FE`, the
  EDITOR's empty pattern `FE` ([section 3](#3-the-editors-own-pattern)), and the defaults of every record the
  user may change (settings, DJ's stand, LOOPS, the MIDI map, GATE).
- **The device never writes FWS.** Only an install does. Its free part, after the firmware's image (52 KB in the
  release build as built now), stays empty: the room the firmware grows into.
- **Every save goes into the user area,** as it does now. A built-in thing that was changed is kept as a record
  in the user area under its own number, found before the firmware's own; BACK TO BUILT-IN deletes that record.
  A built-in thing never moves and has no DELETE.
- **Gone:** MOVE TO FWS, FILL FWS and MOVE TO USER AREA, in every menu; the menus' `... IN FWS` titles
  (`PATTERN 1A IN FWS`, `CELL IN FWS`); the storage-full question's line about moving things into FWS; the
  uploader's offer to put samples into FWS; the store's reading of FWS's free part (direct reads, its scan,
  CHECK STORAGE's check of audio there, a deleted record kept while FWS still holds its object); sample voices
  streamed from FWS (STATUS' SKIPS); STATUS' IN FWS line.
- **Brown numbers** mark only a built-in thing as the firmware has it; changed, it is the user area's.
- **STATUS → STORAGE** shows the user area alone: FWS's line and IN FWS go, as nothing of the user's can be
  there. USER's bar and its free figure are as before, the room kept for the EDITOR's pattern counted as used
  ([section 3](#3-the-editors-own-pattern)).
- **More room** in the user area comes from deleting things, the demo disk's among them.

## 2. Installs

- **The update loader stays as it is.** It writes the whole firmware slot, 0x004000-0x093000, and never the user
  area: installing PT/FM keeps everything the user made, the changed built-ins included.
- **The install page keeps its backup** (before an install, SAVE AS A FILE) for the case where another firmware
  ran on the FM-1 in between: other firmwares write the fast regions too, so for PT/FM their install is the
  loss of everything, and the backup is how it comes back once PT/FM is installed again.
- **PUT BACK INTO FWS goes.** A backup taken from an FM-1 that still held things in FWS's free part (moved there
  before this change) puts them back into the user area after the install, where they fit; what does not fit
  stays in the backup, listed.
- **An install cut short by a power cut does not resume** at the next power-on: the boot code looks for the
  request to run the update loader only in RAM. The USB rescue and the install page bring the FM-1 back. Nothing
  in the specs promises otherwise.

## 3. The EDITOR's own pattern

**What it is.** A pattern that belongs to the EDITOR alone: pattern `FE`, in the firmware's range, empty in the
firmware (8 tracks × 64 rows, EDIT STEP 1, as NEW makes one). (`FF` stays "no pattern" where a song position or a
FUSION slot names none; later built-in FUSION patterns take `F5`-`FD`.)
- **Never listed:** no pattern list shows it (ENV's, SONGS', a FUSION slot's, LOAD A PATTERN's, STORE IN A
  PATTERN's, LOOPS'); no song position or FUSION slot can name it; NEW, CLONE and MOVE never take `FE`.
- **Played and edited as any pattern** while the EDITOR is on it: PLAY, REC, RECORD PATTERN, GLO's properties
  (TRACKS, ROWS, EDIT STEP), the blocks, REVERT.
- **Kept by SAVE,** as a changed built-in is: a pattern record `FE` in the user area. Emptied, its record is
  deleted and the firmware's empty one is back. Switched off without a SAVE, its changes are gone, as every
  change is.
- **Empty** means no cell holds anything (note, instrument, volume or command), whatever its size.
- **The page for the computer** (the editor, its FS) and disks leave it alone, as the keys' song `FE`; the
  install page's backup keeps it, as it keeps every changed built-in.

**Its room.** The store keeps room for the largest pattern: 8 blocks (32 KB), on top of the 2 blocks compaction
needs ([../files/store.md](../files/store.md#garbage-and-compaction)). A SAVE is refused when what it writes, the
EDITOR's pattern apart, would leave fewer than 10 free blocks once compaction has run; the EDITOR's pattern may
use the 8.
- **An amount, not a place:** its record is written into the log at the head like any other, so its blocks take
  their turns with the rest (no wear of their own) and no store laid out before this change has to move.
- **Saved as everything is,** all or nothing: the new copy is written beside the old one, so a power cut leaves
  what the last SAVE wrote. So the 8 blocks hold a full pattern once; rewriting one near its largest while the
  rest of the flash is full needs its room a second time, and that SAVE asks as any SAVE that does not fit
  ([../ui/saving.md](../ui/saving.md#storage-full)): the changes stay in RAM, and once something is deleted SAVE
  writes them.
- **STATUS** counts the 40 KB as used. The uploader's 64 KB kept free covers them.

**What the EDITOR edits** is one pattern at a time: its own, or a numbered one.
- On a fresh FM-1, and whenever the pattern it was on is gone, the EDITOR opens on its own pattern. (Today it
  falls back to the first pattern listed, a built-in FUSION pattern on an FM-1 holding none of the user's.)
- **SAVE keeps which one,** in the settings record (bytes 126-127, below), as it keeps the current instrument:
  moving to another pattern is no change of its own; a SAVE writes it whenever it differs from the one kept.
- **It goes to a numbered pattern** by ENV's pattern list, SONGS' ENV list (`PATTERN 1A FOR THE EDITOR`), EDIT
  PATTERN on a song position, EDIT on PLAYBACK, and a FUSION slot's PATTERN, as today.
- **It comes back to its own pattern** only by NEW PATTERN and LOAD A PATTERN.

**The EDITOR's SEL menu** (the cell's, `CELL`) gains three items, after the block's items and before RECORD
PATTERN and REVERT:
- **NEW PATTERN:** the EDITOR's pattern emptied (8 × 64 again), and the EDITOR on it.
- **LOAD A PATTERN:** the pattern list (titled `LOAD INTO THE EDITOR`, no `+ NEW PATTERN` line); the picked
  pattern is copied into the EDITOR's pattern, and the EDITOR on it. The picked pattern is not changed.
- **STORE IN A PATTERN:** the pattern list (titled `STORE THE PATTERN IN`, with `+ NEW PATTERN` first, the
  pattern the EDITOR is on left out); what the EDITOR shows, its unsaved changes included, is copied into the
  picked pattern (`+ NEW PATTERN`: the first free number), and the EDITOR follows it there. Stored from its own
  pattern, the EDITOR's pattern is emptied; stored from a numbered pattern, that pattern stays as it is, its
  changes included.
- **Storing over a pattern that holds anything** asks first: `PATTERN 0C WILL BE REPLACED` /
  `DO YOU WANT TO CONTINUE?`, YES, NO. Songs that play it hear the new notes at once; REVERT on it brings it
  back until a SAVE.
- Each is an edit like any other: SAVE goes dim, REVERT takes it back. LOAD and STORE are a whole pattern's
  edit, at most ~30 KB of the 63 KB edit memory ([section 4](#4-patterns-at-most-16-tracks--128-rows)); a full
  edit memory asks as for any edit ([../ui/saving.md](../ui/saving.md#the-edit-memory)).

**The warning.** Whenever the EDITOR's pattern holds anything and something would replace it (another pattern
opened in the EDITOR by any of the ways above, NEW PATTERN, LOAD A PATTERN), a question comes first:
`EDITOR CHANGES WILL BE LOST` / `DO YOU WANT TO CONTINUE?`, YES (OCT+), NO (OCT-).
- YES empties the EDITOR's pattern (an edit, saved by the next SAVE) and goes on; NO leaves everything as it
  was.
- So the EDITOR's pattern holds notes only while the EDITOR is on it. (After REVERT ALL brings back a saved one
  while the EDITOR is elsewhere, the next of these asks the same.)
- An empty one never asks.

**On its own pattern the EDITOR shows no number** where it names the pattern:
- the header `EDITOR` (`EDITOR 12` on a numbered one); GLO's title `PATTERN PROPERTIES`, without its USED BY
  line;
- the note editor's where line `ROW 14  TRACK 01`;
- REVERT's question `REVERT THE PATTERN TO SAVED?`;
- PLAYBACK, while the EDITOR's loop plays it: the header `EDITOR`, the footer `ROW 1F`.

**Toasts:** `PATTERN 1A LOADED` after LOAD A PATTERN, `STORED IN PATTERN 1A` after STORE IN A PATTERN,
`NO FREE PATTERN` when `+ NEW PATTERN` finds no free number; NEW PATTERN shows none (the empty grid says it).

**The help** gains a page of the EDITOR's:

```
NEW PATTERNS
{SEL}, NEW PATTERN: START AN EMPTY ONE
{SEL}, LOAD A PATTERN: START FROM A COPY
{SEL}, STORE IN A PATTERN: GIVE IT A
  NUMBER, SO SONGS CAN PLAY IT
{SAVE} KEEPS IT AFTER SWITCHING OFF
OPENING ANOTHER PATTERN EMPTIES IT
  (A QUESTION ASKS FIRST)
```

**The settings record** grows to 128 B: byte 126 bit 0 set, byte 127 holds the numbered pattern the EDITOR
was on at the last SAVE; clear (as in a record from before them), the EDITOR's own.

## 4. Patterns: at most 16 tracks × 128 rows

- **A pattern has 1-16 tracks and 1-128 rows.** The rows byte keeps its meaning (rows − 1); 128-255 are not
  written.
- **Why:** every demo pattern is 128 rows; the largest pattern, every cell full with four commands, is then
  ~30 KB in at most 8 parts of 4 KB (a track's stream at most ~2 KB), so the EDITOR's room is 32 KB, and any
  pattern's whole edit fits the edit memory.
- **What it costs:** 128 rows are 8 bars at 4 rows a beat, 5 at 6, 4 at 8.
- **Where it shows:**
  - GLO's ROWS: 1-128 (by 16 with SEL held);
  - RECORD PATTERN: at most 128 rows;
  - LOOPS: its length at most 128 rows, so 8 bars at 1/16, 5 at 1/16T, 4 at 1/32, until LOOPS becomes a song of
    several patterns ([../open-questions.md](../open-questions.md));
  - FUSION: rows `00`-`7F` are every note, as before; the rows above, kept as a workspace, are gone;
  - the editor page's imports: a module's pattern of more than 128 rows becomes two patterns, one after the
    other in the order, its `Dxx` breaks pointed into the second where they land there (a loop, `E6x`, across
    the split is lost); MIDI files become patterns of at most 128 rows.
- **A pattern record of more than 128 rows** (made before this change) reads as its first 128; its next save
  keeps those.

## 5. The RAM

- **The FWS cache has nothing to hold:** it held only records of FWS's free part
  ([../files/ram.md](../files/ram.md#the-edit-memory-and-the-spare-zone)). Its zone, the data region's last part
  (~88 KB as built), stays where it is, as spare RAM that DJ (13 KB while it plays) and USB audio's buffers
  (6.5 KB) borrow as before; with no records in it, a borrow never refuses. What else it is for is left to a
  later review of the RAM.
