# Proposal: one FAVORITES on the FM-1

Status: **settled** (2026-11-13); its specs are written (2026-11-13) and lead from now on; with `FB` deleted (a
built-in can be), ADD TO FAVORITES is not listed and SEL + GLO says `NO FAVORITES YET`. It changes
[the FAVORITES proposal](202610311523-favorites.md) and the specs it wrote:
[../ui/screens/instruments.md](../ui/screens/instruments.md#favorites),
[../files/instruments.md](../files/instruments.md#favorites), [../editor.md](../editor.md),
[../ui/dialogs.md](../ui/dialogs.md), [../ui/controls.md](../ui/controls.md).

Today the FM-1 holds the built-in `FB FAVORITES` and any FAVORITES cartridges of the user's (NEW CARTRIDGE, NEW in the
cartridge list, the EDITOR, a disk); ADD TO FAVORITES is a group when there are several, SEL + PRESETS turns them in a
favourite's edit, and SEL + GLO falls back to the first of them when `FB` holds none. That is more than the FM-1 needs:
one place to keep the sounds at hand is quicker to reach and to fill, and collections of favourites belong on the
EDITOR's TABLE, where there is room to sort them.

## 1. On the FM-1: `FB` alone

- **The FM-1 holds one FAVORITES cartridge, the built-in `FB`.** Nothing on the FM-1 makes another: NEW CARTRIDGE (the
  INSTRUMENTS SEL menu's group) and the cartridge list's NEW lose their FAVORITES kind, and the cartridge list's CLONE
  is not offered on `FB`.
- **Everything that picked among FAVORITES cartridges takes `FB`:** ADD TO FAVORITES is always an action into `FB`
  (no group); SEL + GLO and GLO + PRESETS use `FB` (with no favourite there: `NO FAVORITES YET`, as now); SEL +
  PRESETS in a favourite's edit does nothing (MIDI's bank select the same).
- **A FAVORITES cartridge of the user's already on a device** (made by an earlier PT/FM) gets no special treatment:
  no conversion, no merge, no test. Whatever the firmware does with it as a cartridge kind it no longer makes is
  fine; the EDITOR shows it on the FM-1 panel as any FAVORITES item and can move it to the TABLE.

## 2. ADD TO FAVORITES and REMOVE FROM FAVORITES

The INSTRUMENTS SEL menu's item, on a slot that holds an instrument (with slots marked: on them), in the same place as
now (right after MARK, first with slots marked; in edit mode too):

- **`ADD TO FAVORITES`** when the slot's instrument is not in `FB` (with slots marked: when any marked one's is not):
  adds it at `FB`'s first empty slot (each missing one, in order, those already there skipped). Toasts as now:
  `ADDED TO FB:07` (the first one added), `FAVORITES FULL` (not enough empty slots: nothing added).
- **`REMOVE FROM FAVORITES`** when the slot's instrument is in `FB` already (with slots marked: when every marked
  one's is): empties every slot of `FB` naming it (each marked one's), no question; toast `REMOVED FROM FAVORITES`.
  `ALREADY IN FB:07` is gone: that case reads REMOVE.
- In `FB` itself the menu keeps REMOVE as now (the favourite at the cursor, or the marked ones).

## 3. On the EDITOR

- **The TABLE holds any number of FAVORITES items,** as now (`+ NEW`'s kind, DETAILS, a disk's).
- **The FM-1 panel shows `FB` alone** among FAVORITES cartridges, as a changed built-in once it holds favourites
  (an earlier PT/FM's user FAVORITES cartridges read from a device are listed as they are, section 1).
- **A FAVORITES item moved to the FM-1 panel replaces `FB`'s slots,** as a system item replaces the panel's
  ([../editor.md](../editor.md#moving-items)): after asking unless `FB` is empty; the cartridges its favourites name
  travel along as now; `FB` keeps its name and number. **Of several moved at once the last one is kept,** in the
  TABLE's order (the bottom-most); a disk's UPLOAD takes its FAVORITES items in their numbers' order, the highest
  kept. The status line names the one `FB` now holds and says the others were not put on (`FB FAVORITES IS NOW MY
  FAVORITES · 2 OTHER FAVORITES NOT PUT ON`).
- **`FB` moved to the TABLE** becomes a FAVORITES item of its own, named `FAVORITES`, as any built-in copied there.
- WRITE writes `FB` as a changed built-in; BACK TO BUILT-IN empties it.

## 4. The playground and the tests

The playground carries the same rules (its INSTRUMENTS menu, NEW CARTRIDGE, the cartridge list, SEL + GLO, GLO +
PRESETS). Tests: ui3 and the playground's (the menu's label both ways, single and marked, REMOVE taking every slot
naming it, NEW CARTRIDGE's kinds, SEL + PRESETS in a favourite's edit, SEL + GLO with `FB` empty); the EDITOR page's
(a FAVORITES item onto the FM-1 panel replacing `FB`, the question, several in one move, a disk's UPLOAD).
