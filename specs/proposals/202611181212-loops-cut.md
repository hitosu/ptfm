# Proposal: CUT a loop

Status: **settled** (2026-11-18) and **built** (2026-11-18); its specs are written and lead from now on
([../ui/screens/loops.md](../ui/screens/loops.md#cut), [../files/loops.md](../files/loops.md),
[../editor.md](../editor.md)). Changed in the build: **SHIFT no longer moves the mark** (section 1 below): the cut is a
window that stays where it is, and at each of its rows the loop plays what it plays there with no cut, SHIFT turning
its notes under it; so a loop is not written out to SHIFT 0 when cut (only its repeats and rest), and the stage over a
cut loop is the loop as heard, REC writing it back with SHIFT 0. The menu item is `CUT HERE` (the toast gives the
place). The cut is stored as section 6 leaves open: a loop's byte 2 bit 6, byte 5 the cut over a base of the length,
once (an older PT/FM reads byte 5 as a repeat count over a base of the length: the loop uncut).

Every loop plays over the one shared LENGTH, its repeats and SHIFT coming back in line at the length's end. **A cut
gives one loop a length of its own:** it wraps at a mark and runs on across the length's end, so it drifts against
the others (loops of different lengths, phasing) and comes back in line only after the least common multiple of the
two. Nothing else changes: the length, the other loops and the loop's notes stay as they are. Its notes past the mark
are kept, unheard; once the cut is removed they play again.

## 1. The cut

- **A loop's cut** is 0 (none, the default) or 1 to the length − 1 rows: the rows before the mark are the loop.
- **It is taken at the current position:** stopped, the play position (where PLAY starts, `PLAY FROM 2.4.3`);
  playing, the row playing. Any row, not only a bar's or a beat's. The rows before it, from the loops' start, are the
  loop's cycle, as they are heard there: its repeats, its rest, its SHIFT. **A loop already shifted is first written
  out as it is heard over the length,** its SHIFT 0, as a length change does, so everything left of the mark loops,
  its wrapped tail included, and it sounds as it did. At the loops' first row nothing lies before the mark: no cut
  there (the menu leaves the item out, the shortcut blinks).
- **It plays** its row (the loops' row − SHIFT) round its cut, the loops' row counted from their start and running on
  across the length's end: PLAY from the start plays the same again. PLAY from a position starts the cut loop where
  the first pass has it there; a jump moves it as far as the play line. A note sounding over the wrap ends there, as at
  the length's end (a note off at the loop's row 0).
- **SHIFT** works as on any loop (SEL held + ALGORITHM, ADJUST: a row a click round the length) and moves the mark
  with the notes: the cycle keeps its notes and starts SHIFT rows later.
- **Its repeats** play as heard up to the mark.
- **A cut is an edit:** SAVE keeps it, REVERT and REVERT ALL undo it, the unsaved count and SAVE's LED take it.

## 2. The keys

- **SEL's menu** on a loop lists `CUT AT 2.4.3` (the current position), and on a cut loop also `REMOVE CUT`. A cut
  loop's `CUT AT` moves its mark there. Toasts: `LOOP 03: CUT AT 2.4.3`, `LOOP 03: CUT REMOVED`.
- **SEL held + EDIT,** the shortcut: tapped, the cut at the current position (a cut loop's moves there); held past
  HOLD DELAY, the cut removed (a loop with none: the button blinks). On `+ NEW LOOP` nothing (SEL blinks), as SEL held
  + ALGORITHM. SEL used so opens no menu.
- Refused during a count-in (the button blinks), as EDIT is; nothing in ADJUST, MOVE or the pattern recorder.

## 3. The stage over a cut loop

The stage follows the selected loop's cut, so a cut loop is overdubbed as it is heard:

- **EDIT** brings onto the stage the loop's rows before the mark, as heard; the stage plays and records them
  wrapping at the mark, drifting with the loop. Its line wraps there, so OVERWRITE erases only those rows.
- **REC** sends the stage into the loop with the cut kept.
- **Moving the selection** to a cut loop while the stage holds notes: the stage wraps at that loop's mark; its notes
  past the mark stay on the stage, unheard, and REC keeps them in the loop.
- **The pattern recorder** fills the stage, so over a cut loop it fills up to the cut.
- On a loop with no cut, or on `+ NEW LOOP`, the stage is the length long, as ever.

## 4. The other tools

- **LENGTH longer:** the cut stays. **Shorter but past the cut:** the cut stays; what the length cuts goes, as from
  any loop. **As short as the cut or shorter:** the cut goes, and the loop plays the new length as any. The question
  (`CUT TO 2 BARS?`) counts every note the change removes, a cut loop's unheard notes past its mark included.
- **DOUBLE** keeps the cut: the loop plays the same.
- **QUANTIZE** (SEL's, on a loop) works on all the loop's notes, past the mark too.
- **MOVE** moves the cut with its loop; **DELETE LOOP** deletes it with it.
- **SAVE TO PATTERN** writes the first pass from the loops' start over the length: the cut loop wraps at its mark as
  heard, its notes past the mark not written. The drift starts over at every repeat of that pattern; RECORD LIVE keeps
  it (its song takes what the player plays).
- **LOAD FROM PATTERN** makes loops with no cut. **STORE AS A SET** and **LOAD A SET** keep the cuts, a part of the
  record.

## 5. The screen

- **The strip** shows a cut loop in full, as it would play with no cut (its repeats, its SHIFT applied). **The mark**
  is a line in the loop's colour in the gap before the mark's cell, a pixel over and under the strip, as the bar lines;
  it moves with SHIFT. **The notes from the mark on** are drawn dimmed, as a silenced loop's. The stage's strip over a
  cut loop likewise.
- **The window and the white play line stay the strips' shared ones.** A cut loop's own place, the row its cycle
  plays, is a second line in its strip, in its colour, while it lies in the window and is not the play line's.
- **The slot's line** marks a cut loop (`CUT` after its repeats and SHIFT, as room allows); the menu and the toasts
  give its place.

## 6. The record

The cut is a field of the loop's entry in LOOPS' record ([../files/loops.md](../files/loops.md#the-record)), 0 for
none, so a record written before reads with no cut. It needs 7 bits; the 12-B entry has one bit never read (byte 2's
bit 6). Where the rest go, spare room elsewhere in the record (version 4 kept) or a new version the EDITOR converts,
is settled with the build. An older PT/FM plays a cut loop uncut.

## 7. The EDITOR, the playground and the tests

- **The EDITOR** reads and keeps the cut. Its WRITE for an FM-1 that reads only version 3 writes a cut loop's first
  pass over the length, as SAVE TO PATTERN does.
- **The playground** twins all of it.
- **Tests:** the wrap and the drift over several passes, PLAY from a position and a jump, SHIFT with a cut, the stage
  (EDIT, REC, OVERWRITE, a selection moved), LENGTH both ways and its question, DOUBLE, MOVE, SAVE TO PATTERN, a set,
  the record read back, RECORD LIVE's song against the player.
- **Room:** the debug build has 2,560 B left at the last build before the cut; the cut must fit it, or room is found first.
