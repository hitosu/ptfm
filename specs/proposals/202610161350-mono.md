# Proposal: the sound made in mono when only mono is heard

Status: **set aside** (2026-10-25; proposed 2026-10-16); nothing of it is built. The estimate and why it waits:
[../open-questions.md](../open-questions.md). It would change how the sound is
mixed and how the effects run while the analog out is mono (OUTPUT = MONO, or SYNC PULSE L / R, whose music side
is mono: [../ui/screens/settings.md](../ui/screens/settings.md),
[../connections.md](../connections.md#clock)), to give the CPU back what the second side costs.

## 1. Why

Today the whole sound is made in stereo, and MONO sums the two sides at the very end, in the output stage. With
nothing but mono heard (the internal speaker, a mono jack, a sync pulse on one side), the work done for the second
side is thrown away. The FM-1's heaviest uses (DJ with its filters, 12 voices) run near the end of the CPU.

## 2. What is mono already, and what is not

**Already mono, so nothing to gain:**

- **The engines.** Every voice is rendered mono and only then panned into the mix: the Juno-106's voices alone take
  about 1,000-1,700 µs of a 5,804 µs half in DJ's heaviest runs, measured on the FM-1.
- **The REVERB's tank.** The plate is fed one signal (both sides summed) and its figure-eight tank is one structure
  (about 570-600 µs a half).
- **The DELAY's line.** One line, fed one signal.
- **The Juno-106's chorus.** Its two sides are two taps modulated in opposite directions: mono still needs both (the
  sum does not cancel).

**Stereo only, and what mono would save (estimated from the stages' figures measured on the FM-1):**

| Stage | Its cost now (a half) | Mono saves (estimate) |
|---|---|---|
| DJ FILTERS: two decks, two sides each, their sends too | about 350 µs | about 175 µs (DJ only) |
| DJ MIX: each deck's strip, a side at a time | about 200-260 µs | about 60 µs |
| The voices' MIX: each voice panned into the two sides, its sends | about 200-360 µs | about 50-90 µs |
| The REVERB and the DELAY back up to 44.1 kHz: a converter a side; the plate's output taps a side | (part of theirs) | about 75 µs |
| The punch-in buffer's sides (their running sums and compensator) | 114 µs, the buffer whole | about 40 µs |
| The LIMITER, the inserts (GATE, FILTER, DRIVE, LO-FI), the output stage | | about 40 µs |

**In all:** about 200-250 µs a half playing a song (about 4 % of the time), about 440 µs on DJ with its filters
(about 7-8 %).

## 3. What it would change

- **The sound, slightly.** Summed first instead of last, the stages that are not linear see the sum instead of each
  side: DRIVE, LO-FI's steps, the LIMITER's detector, the soft ceiling, the clip on the DELAY's line. The linear ones
  (the filters, the delays, the plate) give the same sound up to rounding.
- **What stays stereo:** USB audio carries the stereo mix whatever OUTPUT says, and VISUAL shows it
  ([../connections.md](../connections.md#usb-audio)): while a computer records over USB the stereo path runs.
- **Two paths** through the mixer and each effect, kept in step and tested both ways.

## 4. The ways to do it

1. **A whole mono path:** every stage above, switched at a block's start when the analog out is mono and USB audio is
   not recording. The most saved, the most code.
2. **The large pieces only,** in order of value: DJ's filters in mono (about 175 µs on DJ; the filter is linear, so
   the same sound up to rounding), then one converter each for the REVERB and the DELAY, then the punch-in buffer's
   sides left out. Each measured on the FM-1 before the next.
3. **Nothing:** the saving is small next to the engines' and the REVERB's, which mono does not touch.

**Recommended:** 2, starting with DJ's filters, as a measured trial.

## 5. Open

- Whether the non-linear stages' change of sound in mono is wanted (it is how a mono output would be mixed on
  purpose) or must be avoided (then only the linear stages go mono, and the sum is taken before the first non-linear
  one).
- Switching between the paths while sound plays (OUTPUT changed, USB recording starting): the effects' states of the
  second side, kept or cleared, and whether a click is heard.
- Whether USB audio should follow OUTPUT's MONO too, so the mono path also runs while recording.
