# Proposal: an envelope for FX's FILTER

Status: **settled** (2026-10-16) but for whether the cutoff's 1.45 ms steps are heard, to be listened to on the
FM-1 ([../open-questions.md](../open-questions.md)); its specs: [../ui/screens/fx.md](../ui/screens/fx.md#filters-envelope),
[../files/songs.md](../files/songs.md#the-fx-setup), [../files/commands.md](../files/commands.md#mixer-and-fx-0x80-0xbf),
which lead from here. It gives FX's
FILTER ([../ui/screens/fx.md](../ui/screens/fx.md#the-effects)) an envelope: notes, or the beat, open the filter and
let it close, as a synth's filter envelope does.

## 1. What it is

FILTER keeps its CUT and RESO and gains six knobs: ENV, how far a note opens it; SOURCE, what fires it; and the
envelope's ATTACK, DECAY, SUSTAIN and RELEASE. With ENV at 0 % FILTER is as it is today.

FILTER works on the whole mix (or wherever it sits in the chain), so there is **one envelope**, not one a note.

## 2. Where it rests, how far it opens

- **CUT is where the filter rests** between notes: the mix sounds as FILTER sounds today.
- **ENV is how far a note opens it,** as a part of the way from CUT to fully open, counted in octaves (as CUT
  itself is a log scale): a low-pass opens upward toward LP 20K, a high-pass downward toward HP 20. ENV 100 % opens
  it all the way; 0 % is off.
- CUT at OFF: no filter, and the envelope has nothing to move.

## 3. The envelope: ADSR

- **ATTACK:** from where the envelope is to fully open (by ENV), 2 ms at least, so no hit clicks.
- **DECAY:** down toward SUSTAIN.
- **SUSTAIN:** the level held while the source's note is held, a part of ENV's opening.
- **RELEASE:** from where it is back to CUT, once the source lets go.
- **One envelope for many notes:** each new note of the source starts the attack again from wherever the envelope
  is (no jump, no click); it holds while any of the source's notes is held, and releases with the last.
- **Times in milliseconds,** as on a synth: ATTACK 2 ms to 3 s, DECAY and RELEASE 5 ms to 5 s, each knob's steps
  spaced evenly on a log scale; SUSTAIN 0-100 %.

## 4. SOURCE: what fires it

| SOURCE | Fires | Held |
|---|---|---|
| `KEYS` | each note the keys play: the FM-1's, MIDI's, the arpeggiator's | while the key is held |
| `00`-`0F` | each note that track starts | until the note ends (its track's next note, a note off, NOTE CUT, a FUSION layer's end) |
| `4 BEATS`, `2 BEATS`, `BEAT`, `1/2 BEAT`, `1/4 BEAT` | the beat of what plays, every so many beats, on the beat | half its time (`BEAT`: half a beat; `4 BEATS`: two beats), then RELEASE |

- **KEYS and SETTINGS' KEYS together:** with KEYS DRY ([../ui/screens/fx.md](../ui/screens/fx.md#the-keys-dry)) the
  keys skip the effects but still fire the envelope, so the keys play the filter on the background alone.
- **A track as SOURCE,** heard or not: a muted track's notes still fire it, so a silent track can carry a rhythm
  for the filter.
- **The beat** is what plays's music clock (the song's, the loops', a DJ deck's tempo), as GATE's steps and the
  DELAY's note lengths follow it. With nothing playing it runs on from the last bar at the tempo, as GATE does
  ([../ui/screens/fx.md](../ui/screens/fx.md#gate)): the keys, THROUGH FX, play into a filter pulsing in time.

## 5. The knobs and the picture

- **Two knob pages** on FX LIVE and FX EDIT, ALGORITHM turning them (`ALG 1/2`, as DELAY has):
  - page 1: K1 CUT, K2 RESO, K3 ENV, K4 SOURCE;
  - page 2: K1 ATTACK, K2 DECAY, K3 SUSTAIN, K4 RELEASE.
- **Shown as every knob** in the header: `FILTER ENV 60%`, `FILTER SOURCE 02`, `FILTER SOURCE 1/2 BEAT`,
  `FILTER ATTACK 2 MS`, `FILTER DECAY 1.20 S`, `FILTER SUSTAIN 25%`.
- **The picture** (FILTER's: the response in dB, the cutoff, the peak) moves with the envelope: the response and the
  cutoff line where the filter is now; a dashed line at CUT (where it rests) and one at ENV's reach (light grey and
  yellow); a short tick at the top at each hit.

## 6. Stored, and as commands *(proposed)*

- **Six knobs of the FX setup** ([../files/songs.md](../files/songs.md)), at the free knob codes: 0xB0 ENV, 0xB1
  SOURCE, 0xB2 ATTACK, 0xB3 DECAY, 0xB4 SUSTAIN, 0xB5 RELEASE (bytes 48-53), each a step from its range's bottom:
  ENV and SUSTAIN 0-100; SOURCE 0 KEYS, 1-16 the tracks `00`-`0F`, 17-21 `4 BEATS` to `1/4 BEAT`; the times 0-127.
- **A song saved before reads 0s:** ENV 0 %, the envelope off, so it sounds as it did. A new song's setup: ENV 0 %,
  SOURCE KEYS, ATTACK 2 ms, DECAY 300 ms, SUSTAIN 0 %, RELEASE 300 ms.
- **The commands** take them as any FX knob's ([../files/commands.md](../files/commands.md#mixer-and-fx-0x80-0xbf)):
  a song can change ENV or SOURCE from a row.

## 7. On LOOPS and DJ

- **LOOPS:** its own FX setup has the same knobs. SOURCE keeps a track, as everywhere (a loop's number changes as
  loops are deleted or merged, its track does not), and shows the loop playing there: `LOOP 03`, or `TRACK 05`
  where no loop is.
- **DJ:** each deck plays its song as made, its FILTER and the envelope with it, on that deck's tracks and beat, as
  its GATE, DRIVE, LO-FI and DUCK play; DJ's FX does not show them. SOURCE KEYS does nothing there (the keys play no
  notes on DJ). DJ's own DJ FILTER is another filter after the song's, and gets no envelope: the LFO pads swing it.

## 8. Cost *(proposed)*

- **CPU:** the cutoff already changes a 64-frame block at a time (a coefficient set a block); the envelope adds a
  few multiplies a block, and the source's notes a flag each. The cutoff's glide (~46 ms end to end, against zipper
  noise) stays for CUT turned by hand; the envelope moves it at once, a block at a time (1.45 ms steps), to be
  listened to with high RESO and a 2 ms attack before it is settled (16-frame steps where that is heard).
- **RAM:** a few bytes of state.

## 9. The help *(proposed)*

FX's FILTER lines grow by two: `FILTER ENV: NOTES OPEN IT, ADSR` and `  SOURCE: KEYS, A TRACK, THE BEAT`, on the
page that lists the effects.
