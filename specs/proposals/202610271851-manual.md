# Proposal: the manual, PT/FM OWNER'S MANUAL

Status: **settled** (2026-10-27); its spec: [../manual.md](../manual.md), which leads from now on.

**The manual** tells someone who has just installed PT/FM how it works, and stays the place to look anything up
later. It walks a newcomer through the first hour, then explains every screen, every control, every shortcut and
hold, every message, every setting and every instrument parameter. One hand-written text is built into two outputs:
a page for the computer and a PDF for reading and printing.

## 1. The two outputs

- **The page,** `dist/manual/manual.html`: one self-contained file, opened from the file system as the other pages.
  PT/FM's dark page frame, the contents in a sidebar with a search over headings and text, the text in the middle and
  the FM-1 drawn beside it. **Every key the text names lights on the drawing** when it is pointed at, focused or
  tapped: a single key, a whole combination at once, a row of a key table. The screenshots are in the FM-1's own
  colours. At phone width the drawing sits above the text and the contents become a drawer.
- **The PDF,** `dist/manual/ptfm-manual.pdf`: styled as a device manual of the 1990s. A4; a black band with the
  chapter's number at each chapter's start; side heads in a wide left column; numbered steps with bold numerals; MEMO
  and NOTE boxes in one spot colour; a panel line drawing with numbered callouts; the screens printed as a reflective
  LCD (four greys on a grey-green ground); a ruled footer with the page number and the manual's name; at the back the
  shortcuts, the messages, troubleshooting, the data list, the specifications, the MIDI Implementation Chart and an
  index.
- **The title** is `PT/FM OWNER'S MANUAL`, with `POCKET TRACKER / FUSION MACHINE`, `BY HITOSU` and the firmware's
  version under it (`for PT/FM 0.26`). The manual is the one document that names the version: it describes one
  firmware, and says which.

## 2. What it holds

Front: the cover, the contents, how the manual writes keys, combinations, holds and messages.

1. PT/FM at a glance: what it is, its engines, voices, songs, effects, LOOPS, DJ, MIDI and USB audio; what is built
   in and what the demo disk brings.
2. Installing and updating: the install page, CLEAN INSTALL, uploading the demo disk, USB RESCUE, what an install
   keeps.
3. The panel: every control numbered, the screen's frame.
4. Basic operation: HOME's combination and the screens, the transport, saving and reverting, the help, taps and
   holds, the keys and the current instrument.
5. First steps: a walk-through from playing a demo song to saving a loop.
6. to 15. The screens: SONGS, EDITOR, INSTRUMENTS, PLAYBACK, DJ, VISUAL, STATUS, SETTINGS, LOOPS, FX.
16. MIDI and USB.
17. The EDITOR page on the computer.

Appendices: every shortcut; every message; troubleshooting; the data list (the built-in cartridges, the demo disk);
the specifications; the MIDI Implementation Chart; the index (the PDF; the page has its search); the licences.

Each screen's chapter: what the screen is for, its picture with its parts named, topics with their steps, keys and
messages, and a table of its keys at the end.

## 3. Left for players to find

Two things are never mentioned: FOMA's corners and score (with the touch that steers it), and the header's warning
when the sound takes too much of the chip. FOMA's view itself is described.

## 4. Kept complete by tests

The text is written by hand, for a newcomer, in plain short sentences; it is not generated from the specs, which are
written for building PT/FM. Tests keep it complete: a shortcut, a message, a screen, a setting, an effect or an
instrument parameter the firmware has and the manual lacks fails them, as does a key the drawing cannot light or a
message quoted that the firmware does not show. The screenshots are made from the firmware itself, running on the PC,
at every build, so they never go stale.

## 5. Not in it

The playground and the emulator (a mention where to try PT/FM in a browser at most); documents for developers (the
specs are that); translations.
