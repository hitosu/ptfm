# Proposal: menus and lists in 8 × 16

Status: **settled** (2026-11-13); its specs are written (2026-11-15) and lead from now on
([../ui/dialogs.md](../ui/dialogs.md#rows-in-s)). It changes
[../ui/dialogs.md](../ui/dialogs.md) (menus, properties, the song and cartridge lists, hold layers),
[../ui/screens/settings.md](../ui/screens/settings.md) (the list, the MIDI page), [../ui/frame.md](../ui/frame.md)
(the fonts' uses), and the screens' sketches that show a menu.

Menus, properties (GLO), SETTINGS and most lists draw in FONT_T (6 × 12): small on the FM-1's 1.3-inch screen at
arm's length. **Everything that fits goes to FONT_S (8 × 16),** the header's font: menus at the screen's whole width,
the properties, SETTINGS (its MIDI page too), the song list, the cartridge list, the hold layers that fit. The wide
tables stay in FONT_T: the pattern list (its notes by track), CHECK REFERENCES' list, CLEAN UP's checklist, the
questions' lines, text entry's hints, the FX layer's punch-ins and HOME's layer. PRESETS' lists keep FONT_B.

## 1. The rules

- **A row is 16 px** (the font's height), its text at the row's top + 0; a highlight or a box the row's height.
- **A row too long for one line takes two:** its name left on the first line, its value right-aligned on the second
  (a property, a SETTINGS item, a MIDI mapping: the FM-1's control on the first, the MIDI control and its kind under
  it). Which rows take two is fixed by their texts as drawn, the same in the firmware and the playground.
- **Scrolling goes by rows, a two-line row whole:** the selected row is always shown whole (both its lines), the list
  moving far enough for it at either end; the selection kept in the middle while it can, as now. A two-line row is
  never cut by the list's top or bottom while selected (one not selected may show its first line alone at the
  bottom).
- **The same characters:** FONT_S holds the same 32-95 as FONT_T, so every text draws as it does now.

## 5. The song list and the cartridge list

Their names in FONT_S, their figures in FONT_T beside them (as PRESETS' lists set a name in FONT_B and its type in
FONT_T), rows of 16 px, 10 lines show (the first `max(0, min(sel − 4, n − 10))`), the panel and title as now (title
in S):

- **The song list:** `NR` and the name (16 characters) in S at 8 and 32; the tempo in T right-aligned to 196; the
  length in T at 202 (`01:36`); column heads in T at 38. The playing song green.
- **The cartridge list** (ENV on INSTRUMENTS, NEW LOOP's, K1's in a favourite's edit): the ID and the name (12
  characters) in S at 8 and 32; the type in T at 136 in its engine's colour; the slots used / size in T at 196. Column
  heads in T.

The T figures sit on the S text's baseline (4 px lower in the row).

## 6. Hold layers

A layer goes to FONT_S when every line fits one line of 28 characters and its lines fit the body (11 at most):
SAVE's, GLO's, the LFO layer's `HOME: HELP FOR THIS SCREEN` outside DJ, SEL's where its lines fit (per screen, as
drawn). Otherwise the whole layer stays in FONT_T: HOME's (two columns), FX's punch-ins, the LFO pads on DJ, ARP's.
A layer is never mixed. The panel's height follows its font (`h = 16 × n + 26` in S).

## 7. The playground, the pictures and the tests

The playground draws the same (its `ui.js` menus, properties, lists, SETTINGS, layers). Every shot with a menu, a
properties panel, SETTINGS, the song or cartridge list or a layer open changes: the ui, ui2 and ui3 tests' checks of
positions follow the new tables, their shots are taken again and compared with the playground's; the EDITOR's
pictures of the FM-1, if they draw these, follow; the manual's screenshots are taken again. New checks: a two-line
property selected at the list's bottom shows both lines; the MIDI page's mapping rows; the cartridge list's T figures
on the S baseline.
