# Proposal: connections: USB audio, MIDI, MIDI learn

Status: **settled** (2026-10-10); its specs are written: [../connections.md](../connections.md), SETTINGS'
MIDI page ([../ui/screens/settings.md](../ui/screens/settings.md#the-midi-page)), the settings record's
bytes 115-119 ([../files/settings.md](../files/settings.md)) and the MIDI map record
([../files/midi.md](../files/midi.md)). Kept as the record; the specs lead. Where a detail below says *(a
default)*, it was not decided on its own and can change when tried.

PT/FM talks to the world over USB (MIDI and audio) and the MIDI IN jack. The FM-1 has no MIDI OUT jack.

## 1. USB audio

- **From the FM-1 to the computer only.** The FM-1 is a class-compliant audio input: no driver on
  Windows, macOS, Linux or a phone. No audio comes back.
- **UAC1, 16-bit stereo, at 44.1 or 48 kHz,** the computer picks (phones and many interfaces prefer
  48).
- **Resampled to the computer's clock.** The FM-1's audio runs at its own ~44,145 Hz (+0.1 %). A
  resampler, steered by how full the USB buffer runs, gives the computer exactly its rate, so a
  recording and a live monitor (an app passing the input straight to an output) both run without
  drift or clicks. One resampler serves both rates. Integer arithmetic; its CPU (expected under 1 %)
  is measured on the FM-1.
- **What it carries:** the mix after the effects, without the METRONOME's and the count-in's clicks
  (those are the player's, in the speaker and the jack), at USB GAIN in MASTER's place
  ([../ui/screens/settings.md](../ui/screens/settings.md)), always stereo, after the soft ceiling.

## 2. The USB device

- **No serial port.** A serial console beside the audio makes macOS 13-15 lose the audio input; what a
  console would carry goes over MIDI SysEx, as installs and data do.
- **The USB ID 1209:0001, the product "PT/FM FM-1".** Every FM-1 firmware uses this ID, and the install
  pages find the FM-1 by "FM-1" in its name. The device's version number goes up whenever its
  descriptors change, so computers read them again.
- **The audio control header lists the audio streaming interface before the MIDI one,** for older
  macOS's audio driver *(a default, after another FM-1 firmware's fix)*.

## 3. MIDI in: USB and the jack

Both ports feed one queue; they behave the same.

- **Notes act as the FM-1's keys,** on every screen as its keys do: they play the current instrument,
  write on the EDITOR with REC on, record into LOOPS, go to the arpeggio. Velocity sets the note's
  volume (1-127 as `01`-`7F`); the FM-1's keys, which have none, keep KEYS VOLUME. Every note plays,
  not only the 27 keys' range.
- **MIDI IN CHANNEL:** ALL (the default) or 1-16.
- **Program change** picks the instrument in the current cartridge, as PRESETS does; **bank select**
  (CC 0) picks the cartridge, as SEL + PRESETS does.
- **Pitch bend** bends the notes played from MIDI, ±2 semitones.
- **The sustain pedal** (CC 64) holds the notes played from MIDI while it is down.
- **Every other CC does nothing unless learned** onto an FM-1 control (section 4).
- **The realtime messages** as SYNC says (section 6).
- **The jack's bytes are read by their content, not by the DMA's count** *(a default)*: the count can
  miss a byte, and a note-off then waits for the next note.
- **When the queue runs low, USB waits** (its packets are refused until there is room) rather than
  losing a note-off *(a default)*.

## 4. MIDI learn

**An outside controller's knobs and pads map onto the FM-1's own controls:** its 7 knobs, MASTER, the
14 buttons and the 27 keys. A learned control works exactly as the FM-1's does, on every screen, with
its combinations, holds and layers. MIDI notes need no learning: they play as the keys (section 3).

- **Knobs:** learning tells the kind from the turn made while learning.
  - An **absolute** knob (0-127) gives a click per step of its value, in the direction it moves; its
    first value only sets where it stands, so nothing jumps; at its ends it stops giving clicks.
  - A **relative** knob (an endless encoder: two's complement, offset 64 or sign and magnitude) gives
    its clicks as they come.
  - **MASTER,** a pot, takes an absolute knob as a position.
- **Pads and buttons,** a note or a CC: learning tells the kind from the press made while learning.
  - A **momentary** pad (a message on press and one on release) is held while held, as the FM-1's
    button.
  - A **toggle** pad (one message a press) is a tap on each press.
- **How:** on SETTINGS' MIDI page, EDIT on LEARN: `TOUCH AN FM-1 CONTROL`; the FM-1's control turned or
  pressed, `MOVE THE MIDI CONTROL`; the outside one moved: a toast, `K1: CC 74, CH 1`. HOME cancels. A
  MIDI control learned again replaces its old mapping; an FM-1 control may have several *(a default)*.
- **The mappings,** a list on the MIDI page: SELECT goes through them, LFO deletes the one picked.
- **Kept:** a record of their own (kind 0x31), saved by SAVE like everything else; up to 64 mappings
  *(a default)*.

## 5. MIDI out: USB

- **MIDI OUT:** OFF, KEYS (the default) or ALL.
  - **KEYS:** the FM-1's keys as played, on MIDI OUT CHANNEL (1 at first).
  - **ALL:** the keys, and everything that plays (songs, the loops, the arpeggio): track 0 on channel 1
    … track 15 on channel 16, the volume as velocity.
- **Notes that came in over MIDI are never sent back out** (no MIDI loop). STOP ends every note sent.

## 6. Clock

- **SYNC:** INTERNAL (the default) or EXTERNAL.
  - **EXTERNAL** follows the clock of whichever port sends one, USB or the jack. The tempo comes from it
    (the header shows it); START plays from the song's start, STOP stops, CONTINUE goes on; the **song
    position pointer** moves PT/FM there, so a DAW starts it from any bar.
  - **The clock lost** for half a second: PT/FM plays on at the last tempo, `CLOCK LOST`.
- **CLOCK OUT:** OFF (the default) or ON.
  - **ON:** while PT/FM runs on its own tempo (SYNC INTERNAL), it sends the clock (24 a beat), START /
    STOP / CONTINUE, and the song position when it jumps (another song, a position skipped). Never
    with SYNC EXTERNAL: no clock echoed back.

## 7. SETTINGS' MIDI page

SETTINGS gets a second page, **MIDI**, turned with **SEQ or ALGORITHM** (the header's `SEQ 0/1`, as on
FX): MIDI IN CHANNEL, MIDI OUT, MIDI OUT CHANNEL, SYNC, CLOCK OUT, LEARN, and the mappings. The audio's
LINE GAIN and USB GAIN stay on the first page.

## 8. Not in this proposal

- **Bluetooth MIDI:** not in PT/FM, which never enables the radio: it is reached only through closed
  libraries ([../hardware.md](../hardware.md#the-radio-bluetooth-wi-fi)).
- **Audio from the computer** and **MIDI out on the jack** (the FM-1 has no such jack).
