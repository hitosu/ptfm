# Proposal: the DJ screen

Status: **settled** (2026-10-10), to be built as a **BETA**; the specs lead from here:
[../ui/screens/dj.md](../ui/screens/dj.md) and [../files/dj.md](../files/dj.md). Its few open questions are
in [section 16](#16-open-questions) and in [../open-questions.md](../open-questions.md).

## 1. The idea

**DJ** is a screen for playing live, like a classic DJ stand: two songs on two decks, in time with each
other, mixed with a crossfader, with each deck's own effects. It is only for performing: nothing it does is
written into a pattern or a song.

It ships as a **BETA**, to be tried and reviewed: two songs at once push the FM-1 (one pool of voices, one
REVERB and one DELAY for both), and its header says `DJ (BETA)`.

- **Two decks, the left one the main.** The main's tempo is the set's tempo; the right deck is where the
  next song is made ready. **SWAP** exchanges them without a sound changing.
- **One song list,** and a LOAD key on each deck.
- **A tempo for each deck,** set back to the song's own with one tap; **SYNC** locks the right deck to the
  main, its tempo and its beats.
- **A crossfader,** and a **DJ FILTER** on each deck.
- **Each deck's own effects, from its rows and its engines:** SCRATCH by hand, cues, loops and rolls,
  BRAKE, ECHO OUT, REVERSE, HALF TIME, bar jumps, a KEY shift. They need no recorded audio and no RAM of
  their own. The punch-ins (FX held + a white key) are the effects on the mix.
- **PREVIEW:** headphones on the jack hear the mix in the left ear and the right deck in the right; USB
  audio sends the room the stereo mix all the while.
- **Every function from one tap or one turn,** the ones played live on the note keys (easier to hit than
  the buttons). Combinations are few, and the one that is not a shortcut is a note key with a knob: a
  deck's SCRATCH key held while its knob turns.

## 2. What it has to work with

**The controls** ([../ui/controls.md](../ui/controls.md)):

- **Left of the screen:** MASTER (a pot, the volume), SELECT and ALGORITHM, PRESETS, OCT- and OCT+.
- **Right of the screen:** K1-K4 in a row, then twelve buttons: FX, SEL, ENV, LFO, EDIT, GLO above;
  HOME, SAVE, ARP, SEQ, PLAY, REC below.
- **Below:** 16 white keys and 11 black keys across the whole width. The black keys are grouped 3, 2,
  3, 2, 1 (OP1-OP3, OP4-OP5, OP6-GLO, MONO-POLY, the blank one). **OP6 (F#4) is the exact middle:** it
  sits between white keys 8 and 9, with five black keys on either side. The keyboard splits into two
  halves of 8 white and 5 black keys, one a deck, as a DJ controller has 8 pads a deck.
- **Every screen keeps:** HOME's combination (screens, mutes, help), SAVE, PLAY, HOME + REC (all sound
  off), FX tap and FX + white key's punch-ins, MASTER as the volume.

**The screen:** 240 × 240; T is 40 characters across, two columns of 20.

**Songs** ([../files/songs.md](../files/songs.md)): TEMPO, TICKS A ROW, ROWS A BEAT, their own mixer and
FX setup, tempo and punch-in commands in their patterns, AT THE END (STOP or LOOP). A faster tempo plays
the same notes sooner, not higher: a tracker keeps its key. The player counts ticks exactly, so two decks
at one BPM, started on one bar line, never drift apart.

**The sound** ([../files/ram.md](../files/ram.md), [../ui/screens/fx.md](../ui/screens/fx.md)):

- **One pool of voices,** VOICES 8, 12 or 16; 16 overran once on the FM-1. The demo songs need these at
  most, in each of their six positions, every release counted to −60 dB (as `demo-disk/tools/check.py`
  counts them):

  | Song | Positions 00 → 05 |
  |---|---|
  | PUNCH IN | 7 11 8 12 11 11 |
  | SPICE ROAD | 8 9 10 12 11 12 |
  | MIDNIGHT RUN | 8 10 11 11 10 10 |
  | CITY LIGHTS | 12 13 13 10 15 14 |
  | ACID TEST | 6 7 9 13 12 13 |
  | LEVEL ONE | 6 11 11 6 13 11 |

  One song's last position over another's first needs 16-26: in a blend, notes will be lost.
- **One REVERB (50 KB), one DELAY line (59 KB), one CHORUS:** no room for a second of any. GATE, FILTER,
  DRIVE, LO-FI and DUCK keep only a little state. Five of the six demo songs route CHORUS, DELAY and
  REVERB as sends, with close settings; ACID TEST drives its whole mix; PUNCH IN turns FILTER and GATE by
  commands; three songs pump with DUCK.
- **One punch-in buffer:** the last 0.5 s of the mix, in stereo (88 KB).
- **The tracks:** 16 for a song, and 16 more that LOOPS uses (16-31). The second deck plays on those.
- **The outputs** ([../ui/screens/settings.md](../ui/screens/settings.md)): the speaker and the jack play
  one analog signal (LINE GAIN, MASTER, OUTPUT STEREO / MONO); **USB audio** is a stream of its own, always
  stereo, at USB GAIN, MASTER not applied.
- **The RAM is spoken for:** the edit memory and the slot-sample cache share 63 KB, edits keeping 32 KB
  of it; the cache gives way.

## 3. The screen and what plays

- **DJ is a screen of its own,** with a playback of its own, like LOOPS.
  - **Its key** is the blank one (F#5), beside LOOPS on POLY: the two live screens sit together at the
    keyboard's end, and OP6 stays a HEAR screen's for later.
  - **In the round,** between LOOPS and SETTINGS: … FX → LOOPS → DJ → SETTINGS → STATUS …
  - **Its name** is in the PLAY group's colour, as LOOPS' is; POWER-ON SCREEN can choose it.
- **One thing plays at a time** ([../ui/transport.md](../ui/transport.md)), and the decks are one more: a song,
  the EDITOR's pattern loop, the loops, or the decks.
  - **Opening DJ while a song plays:** the song moves onto the main deck, playing on without a break, in
    place of what was paused there. Only its CHORUS, DELAY and REVERB settings become DJ's
    ([section 6](#6-two-songs-at-once)).
  - **The EDITOR's pattern loop, or the loops,** play on into DJ until a deck starts; they then pause and
    keep their place.
  - **Leaving DJ, the decks play on.** PLAY on the other screens pauses and resumes both; a song, a pattern
    or the loops started elsewhere pause both. The decks and the loops never play together: the right deck
    plays on the loops' tracks.
  - **FX from DJ stays in DJ,** as from LOOPS: it works on DJ's own setup, and FX again goes back.
- **Nothing on DJ is edited:** no pattern, song or instrument changes there. Its only saved state is its
  own setup ([section 12](#12-saving)).

## 4. The decks

### The main and SWAP

- **The left deck is always the main.** Its tempo is the set's tempo; the right deck can follow it (SYNC).
  This is a DJ player's MASTER deck, fixed to a side, so the screen never has to say which one leads.
- **SWAP** (SEQ) exchanges the two decks: their songs, positions, cue points, tempos, filters, KEY, loops,
  mutes, voices. The crossfader is mirrored, and SYNC stays with the side (a right deck synced before is
  synced after), so nothing heard changes.
- **The flow:** the main plays; the next song is loaded on the right, started in time and brought in;
  SWAP; the right deck is free for the next.

### Loading

- **One song list for both decks,** as DJ controllers have one browser: **SELECT** moves its highlight.
  The list marks which songs are on which deck (`L`, `R`, `LR`).
- **A deck's LOAD key** puts the highlighted song on that deck, at its start; the deck's cue point is the
  start, its tempo the song's own, its KEY 0, its filter OFF.
- **While a deck plays, its LOAD is refused** (`STOP THE DECK FIRST`, the key blinking), as a CDJ's load
  lock: a slip of the finger never replaces the song the room hears. A paused deck can be loaded.
- **The same song can be on both decks** (DJs call it playing doubles), to fade from one part of a song
  to another: LOAD it on the right too; while the right deck is paused, a CUES pad puts it (and its cue
  point) at the part wanted; SYNC; PLAY brings that part in on the main's bar line, in phase with the part
  playing; the crossfader moves from one to the other. Each deck reads the song's patterns for itself, so
  the two never wait for each other; both take voices from the pool, as any two songs do.

### The transport

- **The deck's PLAY key** plays or pauses that deck. Paused, its notes are let go and its place kept.
  - **A synced right deck starts on the main's next bar line,** its PLAY key blinking while it waits; a
    second tap cancels the start. A bar is the song's BEATS A BAR beats (its METRONOME's), each ROWS A BEAT
    rows.
- **The deck's CUE key,** as a CDJ's CUE:
  - **paused:** sets the deck's cue point where it is, on the bar line at or before it (a bar line, so
    that a synced start lines the phrases up);
  - **playing:** takes the deck back to its cue point, paused there;
  - **held while paused at the cue point:** plays from it while held, back to it when let go (with
    PREVIEW on, in the right ear).
  - PLAY on a synced right deck then brings the song in from its cue point, on the main's bar line.
- **The PLAY button** pauses and resumes the whole set: both decks together, still in sync. **Held,** it
  takes both back to their cue points, paused (`BACK TO THE CUES`); so does HOME + PLAY.
- **A song's end:** it stops or loops, as its AT THE END says. Through its last 8 bars the deck's time left
  is red, its overview's last bars red, and its PLAY key blinks on the beat, as CDJs warn before a track
  ends.

## 5. Tempo and SYNC

- **A deck's tempo is a share of its song's own,** as a turntable's pitch fader is.
  - **Shown** as the BPM it plays (`124.0 BPM`) and the change (`+3.2%`).
  - **The song's own tempo commands** still act, scaled.
  - **Range:** half to double the song's tempo (a DJ player's WIDE), in steps of 0.1 BPM; a fast turn
    takes bigger steps, as every knob does; SEL held: 1 BPM.
  - **LFO** after a turn sets it back to the song's own (`TEMPO 120.0 BPM: THE SONG'S`).
- **SYNC** (the right deck's key) locks the right deck to the main:
  - **its tempo** is the main's BPM, or its half or double, whichever is nearest the right song's own
    (a 70 BPM song beside a 140 BPM one plays at 70). Its song's tempo commands are overridden while
    synced. It follows the main whether the main plays or not.
  - **its beats** line up with the main's while both play. Switched on while both play, the right deck's
    place moves at once by at most half a beat, so its beats fall on the main's (a note or two may be
    skipped or repeated, as on real players); started while the main plays, it waits for the main's bar
    line. Beats line up across songs with different ROWS A BEAT; the bar line is the main's.
  - **the knobs:** while synced, the right deck's tempo knob does nothing (`SYNC: TEMPO ON THE MAIN DECK`),
    so the right hand never moves the set's tempo by accident; the main's knob turns both.
- **What it follows from real players** (Pioneer's CDJ-3000, rekordbox, Serato, Traktor): a tempo fader as a
  share, with the key kept; a master deck others sync to in tempo and phase, the phase snapped at once; a
  synced deck's fader disabled (as the CDJs do). **Where it differs:** a synced start waits for the bar line,
  where a real player starts at once on the beat and the DJ picks the downbeat by ear in headphones.

## 6. Two songs at once

### Voices

- **One pool, VOICES as set. When it is full, the deck lower in the mix gives way.** A note that finds no
  free voice takes one from the deck the crossfader favours less: that deck's releasing voices first, then
  its oldest; only when it has none left, its own deck's.
- **A deck faded out completely** (the crossfader at the other end) keeps time and takes free voices, but
  never one from the deck heard, so most of its notes are sounding when it is brought in. With PREVIEW on,
  the right deck counts as heard: it still gives way first, but is not limited to free voices.
- **The CPU** stays what one song costs at that VOICES; a song alone on DJ sounds as it does on SONGS.
- **DJ's VOICES** (GLO): AS SET (the global setting, the default), 16, or 24. 24 is for trying: 16 overran
  once on the FM-1, and 8 more voices take roughly 8-12 KB of RAM from the slot-sample cache. The header's
  CPU bar and STATUS's LATE show what it costs.

### Effects

- **Each deck plays its song as made, but for the three big effects:**
  - its song's mixer (levels, pans, DUCK, the sends) and its `=` commands;
  - its song's GATE, FILTER, DRIVE and LO-FI and their `#` commands, on its own sound;
  - a **DJ FILTER** of its own (the deck's filter knob: low-pass one way, high-pass the other, OFF in the
    middle), after the song, before the crossfader.
- **CHORUS, DELAY and REVERB are one each, shared,** as sends that both decks' tracks feed, with **DJ's own
  settings.** A song in GLOBAL routing sends its whole mix to the ones it has on, at their MIX. Its `#`
  commands for those three are skipped.
- **After the crossfader:** the punch-ins and DJ's LIMITER, so two songs together never clip.
- **DJ's FX setup** (as LOOPS has its own, [../ui/screens/fx.md](../ui/screens/fx.md)): CHORUS, DELAY,
  REVERB, LIMITER and the METRONOME (TEMPO FOLLOW is the main's tempo). FX from DJ shows LIVE and EDIT on
  them, the other effects not shown (they are the decks' songs'); its ROUTING is SENDS; there is no MIXER
  page (the decks play their songs' mixers). It starts with the sends as most songs use them: DELAY 1/8.,
  PING-PONG, FEEDBACK 40 %, MIX 30 %; REVERB SIZE 70 %, MIX 35 %; LIMITER on.
- **Punch-ins:**
  - **FX held + a white key** runs the punch-ins on the mix, as on every screen: DJ's beat effects, from the
    0.5 s buffer of what is heard. No new RAM.
  - **A song's own punch-in commands** play while only its deck is heard (the crossfader at its end); while
    both are heard they are skipped: they would hit both songs.
- **What differs from SONGS:** the reverb, delay and chorus are DJ's settings, not the song's; an effect after
  the sends in a song's chain (ACID TEST's DRIVE) shapes only that deck's dry sound.

### PREVIEW and USB audio

- **PREVIEW** (the main deck's OP3 key) turns the jack into a DJ mixer's split cue: **the left ear plays the
  mix, the right ear the right deck alone,** each in mono.
  - The right ear's sound is the right deck after its own effects and its DJ FILTER, before the crossfader,
    without the shared CHORUS, DELAY and REVERB.
  - OUTPUT MONO does not apply while PREVIEW is on: the split always goes to the jack's two sides. The
    speaker would play both ears summed: PREVIEW is for headphones.
- **USB audio always carries the stereo mix,** at USB GAIN, PREVIEW or not. With PREVIEW on, **MASTER is
  the headphones' volume** (it never reaches USB audio), and the room's level is USB GAIN: the FM-1 on a
  computer feeding the speakers, headphones in the jack.
- **Without USB audio,** PREVIEW is for practising on headphones.

## 7. Playing the decks

All from the sequencer and the engines, each on one deck alone; none needs recorded audio.

### SCRATCH

- **Hold the deck's SCRATCH key and turn its tempo knob** (K1 the left, K4 the right): the hand on the
  record. One click is one row, at the hand's speed, the row's ticks spread over the time between clicks;
  turning back plays the previous rows as if they came next (as PLAYBACK's SELECT does). Held still, the
  notes ring, as in a step.
- **Let go, and the deck lands where it would have been:** the song kept time underneath, so a synced deck
  stays in phase.
- **On a paused deck** it moves the deck by hand, the rows heard, to find a point for CUE.

### The pads

The deck's 8 white keys (the left deck keys 1-8, the right 9-16), in one of three modes; **OP6** turns the
mode, for both decks: CUES → LOOPS → FX. **OCT- / OCT+** turn the pads' page where there are more than 8
(positions 08-0F and on).

- **CUES:** pad *n* is the song's position *n* of the page. A tap jumps there on the next bar line, in
  time; paused, the deck goes there, its cue point with it. A tracker's positions are its sections (the
  demo songs' are 8 bars each), so the drop is a tap away and nothing has to be set.
- **LOOPS:** 4 BARS, 2 BARS, 1 BAR, 1/2 BAR | 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32.
  - **A tap is a LOOP:** from the last beat (for 1/4 and longer) or the last step of its size, the rows play
    to the loop's end and round again until the pad is tapped again; the deck then plays on through the
    loop's end. A tracker loop replays the rows themselves, so it never clicks; it stretches an outro under
    the next song.
  - **Held, it is a ROLL:** the same while held; let go, the deck lands where it would have been.
- **FX:** BRAKE, ECHO OUT, REVERSE, HALF TIME | BAR ◀, BAR ▶, KEY −1, KEY +1.
  - **BRAKE** (a tap): the deck's rows slow and all its notes' pitch falls together, to a stop in 0.5 s, and
    the deck is paused there: a turntable's STOP made by the engines, as one bend over the deck's voices.
    PLAY brings it back with a spin-up of about 0.1 s.
  - **ECHO OUT** (a tap): the deck's whole sound goes into the DELAY (DJ's settings, on for its echoes even
    when the DELAY is off); the deck stops on the next beat, and its echoes ring on under the other deck.
  - **REVERSE** (held): the rows run backwards in time, each note still forwards (the song played backwards
    note by note); let go, the deck lands where it would have been.
  - **HALF TIME** (held): the rows at half speed, the key kept; let go, the deck lands where it would have
    been.
  - **BAR ◀ / BAR ▶** (a tap): on the next beat the deck jumps a bar back or on, in phase: phrases started a
    bar apart line up.
  - **KEY −1 / KEY +1** (a tap): the whole deck a semitone down or up (its notes, its samples too), −6 to
    +6, for mixing in key; LFO sets it back to 0.
- **"Where it would have been":** while a SCRATCH, a ROLL, REVERSE or HALF TIME runs, the deck's place
  goes on counting at its tempo (its song's tempo commands among the rows it passes counted too); letting
  go jumps there, as a song position's jump does.

### Mutes

**HOME's combination** mutes the decks' tracks, as on every screen: white keys 1-8 the left deck's tracks
`00`-`07`, keys 9-16 the right deck's (the pads' page: `08`-`0F`). Dropping a song's drums or bass this way
is a tracker's own kill switch. Solo is HOME, SEQ, a white key, within its deck. Mutes are performance
state; SWAP takes them with their decks.

## 8. Controls

**Knobs:**

| Knob | On DJ |
|---|---|
| SELECT | the song list |
| ALGORITHM | the crossfader: the left deck ← → the right deck, 32 clicks end to end (a fast turn takes bigger steps); LFO after it: the middle |
| K1 | the left deck's TEMPO, the set's; with the left SCRATCH key held, its platter |
| K2 | the left deck's DJ FILTER (SEL held: steps of 10) |
| K3 | the right deck's DJ FILTER |
| K4 | the right deck's TEMPO (nothing while synced); with the right SCRATCH key held, its platter |
| PRESETS | the current instrument, as on every screen (the keys do not play it on DJ) |

**Black keys:** each deck's five, mirrored, PLAY outermost; the mode of the pads between them.

| Left deck | Right deck | Does |
|---|---|---|
| F#3 (OP1) | F#5 (the blank key) | PLAY / pause the deck |
| G#3 (OP2) | D#5 (POLY) | CUE |
| A#3 (OP3) | C#5 (MONO) | the left: **PREVIEW** on / off; the right: **SYNC** on / off |
| C#4 (OP4) | A#4 (GLO) | SCRATCH: held, with the deck's tempo knob |
| D#4 (OP5) | G#4 (PIT) | LOAD the highlighted song |
| F#4 (OP6), the middle | | the pads' mode: CUES → LOOPS → FX |

By touch: on the left, PLAY, CUE and PREVIEW are the group of three, SCRATCH and LOAD the pair; on the
right, PLAY is the lone key at the end, CUE and SYNC the pair before it, SCRATCH and LOAD the two beside the
middle.

**White keys:** the pads, 1-8 the left deck's, 9-16 the right deck's ([section 7](#the-pads)).

**Buttons:**

| Button | On DJ |
|---|---|
| SEQ | SWAP the decks (`SHOW ELSE, QUICK`: the decks shown the other way round) |
| OCT- / OCT+ | the pads' page (and HOME's mutes' tracks) |
| LFO | resets the last knob turned (a tempo to the song's own, a filter to OFF, the crossfader to the middle), or the deck's KEY after a KEY pad |
| GLO | DJ's properties ([section 11](#11-properties)) |
| PLAY | both decks: pause / resume; held: both back to their cue points |
| FX | the FX screen on DJ's setup; FX held + a white key: a punch-in on the mix |
| HOME | its combination, as on every screen: the screens; the decks' tracks' mutes; PLAY: both back to their cue points; REC: all sound off; LFO: the help |
| SAVE | saves (DJ's setup, and what else is unsaved) |
| SEL | SEL + PRESETS, as on every screen; SEL held + a knob: coarse steps |
| ENV, EDIT, ARP, REC | nothing on DJ |

- **Taps and turns reach everything;** the held keys are SCRATCH (a note key with a knob), CUE at the cue
  point, the ROLL and the held FX pads (effects while held, as the punch-ins), and the shortcuts every
  screen has (SEL held, HOME held, FX held).
- **While a deck key is held,** the keys around it work as ever: a ROLL on one deck and a cue on the other
  go together.

## 9. LEDs

Off, dim and full, and blinks, so they read the same through the controller firmware
([../controller.md](../controller.md)), which shows only those: **dim where a key does something, full where
something is on, blinking where something waits, dark where nothing would happen.**

**Black keys:**

| Key | Dark | Dim | Full | Blinking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PLAY | no song on the deck | loaded, paused | playing, brighter on each of its beats: two synced decks pulse together | fast (150 ms): a synced start waiting for the bar line; slow, on the beat: the last 8 bars |
| CUE | no song | a song on the deck | paused at its cue point; held, playing from it | slow (500 ms): paused away from the cue point (a tap would set it there) |
| SYNC | no song | off | synced | |
| PREVIEW | | off | on | |
| SCRATCH | no song | ready | held | |
| LOAD | the deck plays: it cannot be loaded | it can take the highlighted song | | fast: a load refused |
| OP6, the pads' mode | | always | | |

**Pads:**
- **CUES:** dim for each position the song has on this page; full where the deck plays (or stands,
  paused); dark past the song's end; blinking: a jump waiting for the bar line.
- **LOOPS:** dim; full while that loop or roll runs.
- **FX:** dim; full while the effect runs (BRAKE until the deck stops, REVERSE and HALF TIME while held,
  ECHO OUT while its echoes ring); KEY −1 or KEY +1 full while the deck is shifted that way.
- The left deck's pads 1-4 sit under the black group of three, 5-8 under the pair: found by touch.

**Buttons:**
- **PLAY:** green while a deck plays, brighter on the main's beats; red full while the set is paused; red
  dim at the start ([../ui/transport.md](../ui/transport.md#plays-two-leds)).
- **OCT- / OCT+:** dim when there is a page that way (positions or tracks past 8), dark when not: a song's
  further cues can be seen.
- **SEQ** dim while a deck has a song; **GLO, LFO, FX, SEL, HOME** dim; **SAVE** by its own rule; **ENV, EDIT,
  ARP, REC** dark.

**The layers are every screen's:** HOME's combination (the black keys the screens; the white keys the
tracks, dim while they play, dark while muted, full while soloed: 1-8 the left deck's, 9-16 the right
deck's); FX held (the punch-ins).

At a glance: which deck plays and whether the two are in step (the PLAY keys pulsing together), which deck
can take a song (its LOAD dim), where each song is (the bright cue pad), what is about to happen (anything
blinking).

**The state line,** one thing at a time, the first that applies: `SCRATCH`, `ROLL 1/16`, `LOOP 1 BAR`,
`BRAKE`, `ECHO OUT`, `REVERSE`, `HALF TIME`, `STARTS AT THE BAR`, `JUMP AT THE BAR`, `AT THE CUE`; else the
filter, `FILTER OFF`, `LP 2.4K`, `HP 400` (as FILTER's CUT, [../ui/screens/fx.md](../ui/screens/fx.md)).

**A deck with no song:** `NO SONG` on its song line, then `OP5: LOAD THE` and `HIGHLIGHTED SONG` (the right
deck: `PIT: LOAD THE`) on its place and overview lines; nothing else.

### The overview

A tracker's stand-in for a DJ player's waveform. **Each bar of the song is a column as tall as it has notes**
(notes started in the bar, every track, 2-14 px from the bottom, scaled to the song's busiest bar), read from
the song's patterns once at LOAD (about 120 B a deck). Dense parts stand tall, intros and breakdowns sit low:
the drop can be seen coming.

- A bar's columns: `x0 + floor(b × 114 / bars)` to the next bar's.
- **Over it:** the positions' boundaries (1 px, black), the playhead (1 px, white, y 60-76), the cue point
  (a yellow mark 3 × 2 at y 59), a loop's span (2 px, green, under the columns).
- **The played part** dark grey, the rest light grey; **the last 8 bars** red while their warning runs.

### Colours

- **The header:** `DJ (BETA)` in the PLAY group's colour; the pads' mode (`CUES`, `LOOPS`, `FX`) white; the
  pads' page, where there is more than one, light grey.
- **A deck:** the song's number dark grey, its name white, green while the deck plays; `NO SONG` dark grey,
  the hint's `OP5` and `PIT` yellow; the tempo white, `BPM` light grey, the change light grey (white when
  not 0), `SYNC` green; `POS` and `BAR` cyan, their numbers light grey; the beats deep green, the one playing
  yellow on the bar's first beat and green on the others; the time left light grey, red in the last 8 bars;
  the state line yellow for what runs, light grey for the filter (white when not OFF), `KEY +1` white.
- **The crossfader:** `L` and `R` cyan, the track dark grey, the mark white; `PREVIEW` yellow.
- **The list:** the column names cyan; a deck's mark green while that deck plays the song, else light grey;
  numbers dark grey; names light grey, the highlighted one white in a white box; BPM, positions, length light
  grey.
- **The divider and the rules** dark grey.
- **The footer's right:** the voices light grey, red for a second after a note was taken from the quieter
  deck; a knob's name and value white.

## 12. Saving

- **SAVE keeps DJ's own setup:** its FX setup and its properties, in a record of its own: **kind 0x23,
  number 00** (~80 B: the FX setup as a song's, 64 B; VOICES, CROSSFADER, FILTER RESONANCE; spare), saved like
  any change ([../files/store.md](../files/store.md)). Changing them is an unsaved change.
- **Never stored:** the decks: their songs, places, cue points, tempos, KEY, SYNC, filters, loops, the
  crossfader, the mutes, PREVIEW. They are performance state, as mutes are everywhere. After a power cycle DJ
  opens with both decks empty (unless a song plays, which moves onto the main deck).

## 15. Cost and building

**RAM** (estimated, to be measured), about 7-8 KB, from the slot-sample cache, which gives way already:
- the right deck's pattern, read as it plays: 4 KB; its song: under 0.5 KB;
- the right deck's small effects (GATE, FILTER, DRIVE, LO-FI, DUCK) and the two DJ FILTERs: about 1 KB;
- each deck's own mix, which PREVIEW needs: 2 KB;
- the overviews: 0.25 KB.
- The right deck's tracks need nothing new: they are LOOPS' tracks 16-31. VOICES 24 adds roughly 8-12 KB.

**CPU:** the second sequencer is cheap; the voices stay capped by the pool. The right deck's small effects
and the two DJ FILTERs add at most about 6 % with every one of them on (the playground's estimates: GATE
0.4, FILTER 1.2, DRIVE 0.8, LO-FI 0.5, DUCK 0.3); usually the two filters and a song's one or two. The bend
for BRAKE and KEY is a factor a voice a block: next to nothing.

**Building:** in the playground first (the right deck on its player's loop tracks, the pool's priority, the
deck effects, PREVIEW as the page's two channels), then the firmware. **To measure on the FM-1:** CPU, MAX and
LATE with two songs at VOICES 12, 16 and 24; how many notes the quieter deck loses in the demo songs' blends;
how the scratch feels at 24 clicks a turn; BRAKE's and ECHO OUT's sound; PREVIEW on headphones with USB audio
playing the room.

## 16. Open questions

5. **A synced start:** on the bar line, or on the beat as real players; to revisit once tried.
