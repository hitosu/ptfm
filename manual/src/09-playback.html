PLAYBACK

PLAYBACK follows what plays: the pattern playing now, scrolling with the music, in the EDITOR's grid. Nothing on it can be changed by accident, so it is the screen to perform from: jump between positions, change the song's speed and volume, play over it. Open it with HOME + OP4.

What it shows

  • The pattern playing now, scrolling with the play position. There is no cursor and no editing.
  • The header shows the song position and the last one (02/0B); the footer's right side the position and row playing (POS 02 ROW 1F).
  • While the EDITOR's pattern loop plays, the header says PAT 0C (or EDITOR for its own pattern) and the footer PAT 0C ROW 1F.
  • While LOOPS' loops play, each loop is a track; the header says LOOPS. EDIT then goes to LOOPS.
  • When nothing plays, it shows where playback paused, or the current song's start; with no song at all, NOTHING HAS PLAYED: PLAY STARTS THE SONG.

SEQ (or K2) shows 8, 4, 2 or 1 tracks at once, as in the EDITOR (4 TRACKS). ALGORITHM moves the highlighted track across; the view scrolls sideways to keep it in sight.

Playing the song live

  • PLAY pauses and resumes what played last.
  • SELECT steps through the song a row at a time: the first click pauses and plays the current row again; each click forward plays the next row, each click back the previous one. PLAY goes on from there.
  • K1 jumps to another position of the song (K1 POSITION 03/0B PATTERN 05). While playing, the jump comes as the current row ends, and it is a clean start: every note of the song is cut and the effects' tails emptied. Paused, PLAY resumes from that position.
  • K3 is the song's volume, from +6 dB down to -24 dB and OFF, a dB a click (K3 VOLUME -6 DB). It turns the song alone, not the keys, so you can play over a quieter song.
  • K4 is the song's speed, in BPM, from 3 to 600 (K4 TEMPO 124.0 BPM -3.2%: the BPM, and the change against the song's own tempo). Every tempo the song plays is scaled by it, its tempo commands too.
  • LFO puts back the one of K3 and K4 turned last: the volume to 0 dB, the speed to 100 %.
  • The keys play the current instrument over the song; ARP arpeggiates them.
  • HOME + white key mutes and unmutes tracks.
The speed and the volume are not saved. They stay for every song you play until something else plays (the EDITOR's pattern loop, LOOPS, DJ), and at power-off they go back to 100 % and 0 dB.

More from PLAYBACK

  • EDIT opens the EDITOR on the pattern playing, with the cursor on the playing row; HOME comes back here.
  • ENV opens the song list: PLAY there plays the highlighted song from its start, and PLAYBACK follows it; ENV again closes the list.
  • GLO opens the view's options (PLAYBACK VIEW): SCROLL by ROWS (the playing row stays in the middle) or by PAGES (the highlight moves, the pages flip; also SETTINGS' PLAYBACK SCROLL), and UNLOAD SONG, listed once something has played: everything stops, and the keys get their own effects and tempo back, as at power-on (chapter 6).
