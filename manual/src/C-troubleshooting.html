When something is wrong

What to look at first when the FM-1 does not do what you expect, from the sound to the flash. Each answer points to the chapter that tells the whole story.

The sound

What happensWhat to do
No sound at all.Turn MASTER up. A cable in the output jack mutes the speaker: unplug it to hear the speaker. SETTINGS' LINE GAIN sets the level of both (chapter 13).
On the speaker, some parts are missing.The speaker plays one channel only, so a part panned to the other side is not heard. Set SETTINGS' OUTPUT to MONO while you use the speaker, back to STEREO for headphones (chapter 13).
A track is silent.It may be muted, or another track soloed: HOME + white key switches a track's mute. While HOME's combination is open the white keys show the tracks: dim while a track plays, dark while it is muted, bright while it is soloed (chapter 4).
A note or a whole part plays nothing, and its instrument or pattern is drawn in red.It refers to something that is gone (an instrument, a pattern, a sample deleted or moved). SETTINGS, TOOLS, CHECK REFERENCES lists every such place (chapter 4).
The keys are too quiet or too loud against a song.SETTINGS' KEYS GAIN sets how loud the keys sound; KEYS VOLUME sets the volume of the notes they write (chapter 13).
Crackles or clicks in a busy part.Too much is asked of the FM-1 at once: many notes on heavy instruments with several effects on. Set SETTINGS' VOICES to 12 or 8, keep SECOND CORE on, or switch off an effect you do not need (chapter 13). STATUS shows how busy each processor is (chapter 12).
A computer records the FM-1 too quietly or too loud.SETTINGS' USB GAIN sets USB audio's level; MASTER does not touch it (chapter 16).

Starting and installing

What happensWhat to do
The screen says PT/FM CRASH.PT/FM stopped on an error. The FM-1 restarts by itself after a few seconds; what was saved is kept, edits not saved are lost. Until you switch the FM-1 off and on, it runs on one processor (VOICES 16 plays 12 voices at most; STATUS' DEVICE says STOPPED: AFTER A CRASH). If it happens again, note the numbers on the red screen.
The FM-1 does not start, or starts in USB RESCUE.After two failed starts in a row the FM-1 waits in USB RESCUE for an install. Install the firmware again from the install page (chapter 2). To reach USB RESCUE yourself, hold OCT- while switching on.
An install was cut short (a cable pulled, the power lost).It does not go on by itself. Press INSTALL FIRMWARE again, from USB RESCUE if need be (chapter 2).
The install page finds no FM-1.Close whatever else uses its MIDI port (a music program, another installer, another browser tab) and try again; use a USB cable that carries data (chapter 2).
An upload is refused with SAVE OR REVERT FIRST.The FM-1 has edits not saved: press SAVE (or revert them), then upload again (chapter 2).
The FM-1 shows DATA NOT RECEIVED: TRY AGAIN after an upload.The upload stopped half way. What was there before is whole, unless a nearly full FM-1's library was being replaced; either way, upload again.

Saving and storage

What happensWhat to do
Work is gone after switching off.Only what SAVE wrote survives power-off. The SAVE light is dim while something is not saved (chapter 4).
MEMORY FOR EDITS IS FULLThe working memory for changes is full. Answer SAVE NOW (OCT+), then make the edit again. STATUS' EDITS FREE shows the room left (chapter 12).
NOT ENOUGH STORAGE TO SAVEThe flash is full. Answer CLEAN UP (OCT+) to delete what nothing uses, or delete songs, patterns or samples yourself, then save again; nothing is lost meanwhile. STATUS' FLASH FREE shows the room left (chapter 13).
SAVE FAILEDSave again. If it keeps failing, run SETTINGS, TOOLS, CHECK STORAGE, which lists anything damaged (chapter 13).
You want to start from nothing.SETTINGS, TOOLS, FACTORY RESET erases everything you saved or uploaded; the built-in instruments stay. Download a disk first on the editor page to keep a copy (chapter 13, chapter 17).

MIDI and sync

What happensWhat to do
A keyboard plays nothing.Check SETTINGS' MIDI IN CHANNEL: ALL hears every channel, a number only that one. A keyboard's DIN cable goes into the MIDI IN jack through a TRS-to-DIN adapter, of type A or B (chapter 16).
An outside knob does nothing.Only notes, program change, bank select, pitch bend and the sustain pedal work by themselves; learn any other control onto an FM-1 control first (chapter 16).
PT/FM does not follow a sequencer's clock.Set SETTINGS' SYNC to EXTERNAL, and let the sequencer send its clock to the FM-1, over USB or the jack (chapter 16).
CLOCK LOSTThe outside clock stopped for half a second; PT/FM plays on at the last tempo. Start the clock again (chapter 16).
A computer gets no notes from the FM-1.Set SETTINGS' MIDI OUT to KEYS or ALL; MIDI out goes over USB only (chapter 16).
