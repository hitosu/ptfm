First steps

Half an hour with PT/FM: play a demo song, play the keys, write a pattern, record a loop, and save. The demo disk should be on the FM-1 (chapter 2).

Play a demo song

  1. Switch the FM-1 on. It starts on STATUS, which shows the version and how to get help.
  2. Press HOME + OP1: SONGS, the list of songs.
  3. Turn SELECT to a song, then press PLAY. Turn MASTER for the volume.
  4. Press PLAY again to pause; hold it to stop and go back to the start.
The FM-1's speaker plays one channel only, and PT/FM sends stereo at first, so a part panned to the other side is not heard on it. To hear the whole mix on the speaker, set SETTINGS' OUTPUT to MONO; set it back to STEREO for headphones. A cable in the output jack mutes the speaker.

Play the keys

  1. Play the keys: they play the current instrument, named in the footer's left corner.
  2. Turn PRESETS to try the instruments of its cartridge.
  3. Hold SEL and turn PRESETS to change the cartridge: try F8, the JUNO-106, or FA, the TB-303.
  4. Press OCT- or OCT+ to play lower or higher.

Write a pattern

  1. Press HOME + OP2: the EDITOR, with its own pattern. Tracks go across, rows go down; each row is a step in time.
  2. Press REC: its light comes on, and the keys now write.
  3. Play a key: the note is written at the cursor with the current instrument, and the cursor moves down. Play more. SELECT moves up and down, ALGORITHM to another track; LFO clears a cell.
  4. Press REC to stop writing, then PLAY: the pattern plays in a loop from the cursor's row. To hear it from the top, turn SELECT back to row 00 first.

The loop plays at the current song's tempo and through its effects: the song at the cursor on SONGS. To make it faster or slower, change that song's TEMPO (SONGS, GLO), or make the pattern a song of its own, as below.

The EDITOR's own pattern is a sketch pad. To use it in a song, give it a number: SEL, STORE IN A PATTERN (chapter 7).

Make it a song

A song is a list of patterns played one after another, its order. From the pattern you wrote to a song of your own:

  1. In the EDITOR, press SEL and pick STORE IN A PATTERN, then + NEW PATTERN: your pattern gets the first free number (STORED IN PATTERN 00), and the EDITOR follows it there.
  2. Press HOME + OP1 for SONGS. Press SEL and pick NEW: the pattern list asks what the song starts with (NEW SONG STARTS WITH). Turn SELECT to your pattern and press SEL. The song, NEW SONG, is added at the end of the list and becomes the current song.
  3. Press EDIT to go into its order. SEL opens the position's menu: CLONE PATTERN puts a copy of the pattern after it, to vary; NEW PATTERN puts an empty one after it. K1 changes which pattern a position plays, and EDIT PATTERN in the menu opens it in the EDITOR (HOME comes back). Press EDIT again when the order is done.
  4. Press GLO for the song's properties: set its TEMPO with K1 (the BPM it makes is shown beside it), give it a NAME (EDIT types it), and choose whether it loops or stops AT THE END. GLO closes.
  5. Press PLAY to hear the whole song, then SAVE.

Record a loop

  1. Press HOME + POLY: LOOPS. The cursor is on + NEW LOOP.
  2. Pick a sound with PRESETS, then press REC.
  3. Play: recording starts with your first note (or press PLAY to start it on the beat).
  4. Press REC again: the loop closes at the next bar line and plays on. Its length is now every loop's length.
  5. Pick another sound and press REC again: the next loop starts with the loops' next round.

Save

Everything you did is in the FM-1's working memory: the SAVE light is dim. Press SAVE. When the music plays, the save waits for a pause; press PLAY to pause and it is written: SAVED. Now your pattern and loops survive switching off.
