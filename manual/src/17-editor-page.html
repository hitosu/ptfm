The EDITOR page

The EDITOR page, editor.html, is PT/FM's page for the computer (Chrome or Edge, offline). It comes with PT/FM, as install.html does: get it from PT/FM's home, hitosu.github.io/ptfm. It backs up the FM-1, brings new content to it (tracker modules, MIDI files, DX7 cartridges, sounds), and edits it with the FM-1's own pictures. It is a beta: its two panels are finished, its edit tabs work with plain controls.

Two panels

  • TABLE (left): everything you drop on the page, kept in the browser. Its items have no numbers, so it holds any number of songs, patterns and cartridges, and two imported modules never clash.
  • FM-1 (right): an image of what an FM-1 holds, with its numbers.
  • Drag items between the panels (or use the arrows): an item moves with everything it uses (a song with its patterns, their instruments, their samples), and numbers are given as it lands on the FM-1 panel. DELETE removes items; UNDO takes back any step.
  • Both panels group their items by kind: SONGS, PATTERNS, CARTRIDGES, SAMPLERS, SAMPLES and SYSTEM (the settings, the MIDI map, the loops, DJ's setup, the EDITOR's own pattern and the keys' song). A click on a group's header folds or opens it. Each panel shows its items as DETAILS (a row each, with sizes and what uses them), LIST or ICONS.
  • Picking works as in a file manager: a click picks one item, Ctrl adds one, Shift a range, Ctrl+A all. A double-click (or Enter) opens the item's tab, Space plays it, Delete deletes it, F2 renames it; the right-click menu has the rest.
  • The space bar under the FM-1 panel shows the FM-1's room: 103 blocks of 4 KB, as data, audio and free. Keep at least 16 blocks free for the FM-1's own saves: with fewer than 10 free, the FM-1 cannot save new things (it can still delete), and with fewer than 3 it cannot save at all until a WRITE frees room.

An item's tab

Every item opens in a tab of its own: double-click it, or EDIT IN A TAB in its menu. The tab edits a copy: SAVE puts the changes into the item, on its panel, and closes the tab; closing a tab with changes not saved asks SAVE, DISCARD or KEEP EDITING. A change saved on the FM-1 panel reaches the FM-1 only with WRITE. The pictures in a tab are the FM-1's own, drawn by PT/FM's own code exactly as the FM-1's screen shows them, and they follow the controls as you move them.

Song
Its properties (NAME, TEMPO, TICKS A ROW, ROWS A BEAT, SWING, AT THE END), its order (each position's pattern from a list, moved, removed or added), TRIM (tick the positions to keep), the MIXER (each track's LEVEL, PAN, DUCK and SENDS) and the FX setup (the chain's order by dragging, each effect on or off, its knobs).
Pattern
The grid, made for the mouse and the finger: a click picks a cell, a drag marks a block. The cell's panel has a keyboard of the FM-1's 27 keys with NOTE OFF and NO NOTE, the instrument picked from a list of the cartridges by name, the volume, and up to four commands, each with its meaning in words. On the computer's keyboard, the arrows, Page Up / Page Down, Home, End and Tab move; the letter keys write notes as the FM-1's keys play them (Z S X D C F V ... from F-3); = writes a note off; Delete clears; Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V copy, cut and paste; Space plays.
Instrument
Its NAME, LEVEL (AUTO LEVEL measures it as the FM-1 does) and its engine's parameters, each page beside the FM-1's picture of it. Every instrument page has a keyboard of the FM-1's 27 keys, played by mouse, touch, the computer's keys, or a MIDI keyboard picked from the computer's inputs. With VIRTUAL MIDI KEYBOARD picked, the computer's keys play anywhere in the tab (Z S X D C ... from F-3 as the FM-1's keys; [ and ] change the octave).
Sample
Its waveform with START and END to drag (or drag across it for a new cut), PLAY THE CUT, CHANNELS (both mixed, left or right of a stereo sound), RATE, DEPTH (8 or 16 bits), TRIM SILENCE, WHOLE SAMPLE, the sizes before and after, and the instruments that play it. Their START, END and loop points follow a cut when you SAVE. OPTIMIZE (below) makes it smaller.
SETTINGS, MIDI MAP, LOOPS, DJ SETUP
Every setting (and your 16 GATE patterns as rows of steps), each MIDI mapping, each loop's instrument and notes, and DJ's crossfader, filter, decks and effects.

Your own sounds

A sound dropped on the page becomes a sample, a recording. To play it from the FM-1's keys it also needs an instrument that plays it: the instrument sets its note, loop and envelope. The page does not make one for you. On the page:

  1. Drop the sound (WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG ...) on the TABLE.
  2. Drag it onto a sampler cartridge of the FM-1 panel: it joins that cartridge. (Dragged onto the panel anywhere else, it gets a new sampler cartridge of its own.)
  3. Open the sampler cartridge's tab: beside the sample, NEW INSTRUMENT FOR IT makes the instrument. Set its BASE NOTE (the note the recording plays at its own pitch) and its loop, then SAVE.
  4. WRITE.

Or make the instrument on the FM-1, after the WRITE: INSTRUMENTS, the sampler cartridge, an empty slot; SEL, INIT makes a plain instrument there; EDIT, and on its SAMPLE row ENV lists the cartridge's samples (the keys play the one highlighted, ENV picks it). Set its BASE NOTE and loop, then SAVE (chapter 8).

Making room

Samples take most of the FM-1's room. The page can make them smaller: in a sample's tab, OPTIMIZE offers three levels, each with its size and what it saves, heard before you SAVE:

LOSSLESS
Changes nothing you hear: it cuts what no instrument plays (before the first START, after the last END or LOOP END) and the silent tail.
SMALLER
Adds the rate the page proposes for the sound (the lowest that loses nothing heard).
SMALLEST
Adds 8 bits, a softer top, and a level 6 dB lower, made up by the instruments' LEVEL.

On the TABLE, OPTIMIZE SAMPLES does the same to every sample picked (and every sample of a picked sampler) at once, listing each level's size before and after. UNDO takes it back.

Reading, writing and backing up

Connect the FM-1 by USB with PT/FM running. Nothing stays connected: each action finds the FM-1, does its work and lets go.

READ
Reads what the FM-1 holds into the FM-1 panel (after the first time, only what changed).
WRITE
Makes the FM-1 hold exactly the panel. Before writing it shows what it writes and what it removes (in red), warns when the FM-1 saved something since the last READ (READ FIRST, WRITE ANYWAY or CANCEL) and when fewer than the 16 advised blocks would stay free, and offers to back up first: what the write would replace goes onto the TABLE (BACKUP and the time). The FM-1's sound stops while it writes. With unsaved edits on the FM-1 it is refused: SAVE OR REVERT FIRST.
REVERT
Puts back what the panel showed after the last READ or WRITE.
DOWNLOAD DISK
Saves the FM-1 panel as a disk file (ptfm-2026-10-26.ptfmdsk). This is the backup: READ, then DOWNLOAD DISK.
UPLOAD DISK
Puts a disk file in the FM-1 panel, numbers kept; WRITE then puts it on the FM-1. A backup put back is exactly as it was.
Before installing another FM-1 firmware, READ and DOWNLOAD DISK: other firmwares erase PT/FM's memory.

Bringing content in

Drop files on the TABLE (or + ADD FILES):

Disks
.ptfmdsk files: their items as they are.
Tracker modules
MOD, S3M and XM: the song, its patterns (a channel a track, 16 at most), a sampler cartridge with every instrument and sample. IT is not read; XM's envelopes are not kept.
MIDI files
A song: each channel's notes become tracks, notes on the nearest row, the tempo kept, patterns of 4 bars; each channel's instrument mapped onto a built-in sound (all 128 General MIDI programs have one), the drums onto FM DRUM KIT. Pitch bend and controllers are left out.
DX7 cartridges
.syx, the 32-voice dump: a DX7 cartridge, exactly; a single voice's dump: a cartridge of one.
Sounds
WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG and the rest: a sample, at the lowest rate that loses nothing heard.

Take only a part (some patterns, a cut of a sound) in the item's tab, then move it to the FM-1 panel and WRITE.
