Every message

The messages the FM-1 shows, in the header for two seconds (toasts) or in a question, grouped by where they come up, with what each means and what to do. Numbers and names in them change with the case.

On every screen

MessageMeans
OCT +3The keys' octave changed (OCT- / OCT+).
TRACK 02 MUTEDHOME + a white key muted that track.
TRACK 02 ON... and unmuted it.
TRACK 02 SOLOThe track is soloed (HOME + white key held, or SEQ first).
SOLO OFFThe solo ended: every track plays again.
BACK TO THE STARTHOME + PLAY, or PLAY held: back to the start.
NO SONG TO PLAYPLAY with no song (or a song with no positions): make one on SONGS, or upload the demo disk.
SOUND CUT: LET GO OF RECHOME + REC: all sound is off while REC is held.
SOUND CUT, EFFECTS OFFREC held 2 s: every effect of what plays switched off (SAVE keeps it, REVERT undoes it).
ALL SOUND OFFEvery note and effect tail stopped at once.
CHANGES DROPPEDHOME left an edit, throwing its changes away.
SELECT MOVES IT, SEL PUTS IT DOWNA MOVE started: carry the thing with SELECT, put it down with SEL, HOME takes it back.
NOT MOVEDHOME during a MOVE: it is back where it was.
SELECT, ALG: MARK SEL: ITS MENUMARK started: stretch the mark with SELECT and ALGORITHM, then SEL for its menu.
OCT+ OCT-Over a question's answers: the key that gives that answer (OCT+ yes, OCT- no).
ARP UP C-4The arpeggiator on, with its pattern (and, on FX LIVE, the note it plays hands free).
ARP OFFThe arpeggiator off.
LATCH 4 NOTESARP held + K1: how many notes go on after you let go.
LATCHED NOTES LET GOARP held + LFO: the latched notes stopped.
SECOND CORE STOPPEDThe second processor stopped and one core does it all (VOICES 16 counts as 12). It comes back at the next power-on; STATUS' DEVICE says why.
NOT NOW: THE FLASH IS BUSYThe FM-1 is writing its memory: try again in a moment.
TOO BIG FOR ONE RECORDThe thing would be too big to keep in one piece: make it smaller (fewer tracks or rows).
