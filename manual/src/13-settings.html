SETTINGS

SETTINGS holds the FM-1's global settings and the tools that look after your storage. It has two pages: the list, and MIDI. Open it with HOME + MONO (or EDIT on STATUS).

Changing a setting

  1. SELECT picks a setting; the footer shows its default (DEFAULT STEREO).
  2. K1 changes it, a step a click. It applies at once.
  3. LFO puts it back to its default.
  4. SAVE keeps the settings after power-off, as every change.

SEQ or ALGORITHM turns to the MIDI page and back. SEL, REVERT takes the page back to what was saved, after a question (REVERT THE SETTINGS TO SAVED?); with nothing unsaved, NOTHING TO REVERT.

Sound and output

OUTPUT
What the jack and the speaker get. STEREO for headphones and a mixer. MONO for the FM-1's own speaker, which plays one channel only (and for a mono cable). SYNC PULSE L or R: a click on that side every half beat, for instruments that follow a sync pulse, the music in mono on the other side (with SYNC INTERNAL only; with SYNC EXTERNAL the output stays stereo, no clicks). A cable in the jack mutes the speaker, and the FM-1 cannot tell, so set this yourself. STEREO, MONO, SYNC PULSE L, SYNC PULSE R; STEREO at first
LINE GAIN
The level of the speaker and the jack, with MASTER. -24 to +12 dB, 0 dB at first
USB GAIN
The level of USB audio, the sound a computer records; MASTER does not touch it. -24 to +12 dB, 0 dB at first
VOICES
How many notes are heard at once, everywhere. 16 needs the second processor (SECOND CORE); while it does not run, 16 counts as 12 (16 VOICES: 12 WITHOUT CPU 2). When all are busy, a new note takes the voice least missed: a ringing tail first, then the quietest held note; the keys' notes go last. A held note pushed out comes back when a voice frees. 8, 12, 16; 16 at first
SECOND CORE
The FM-1's chip has two processors: ON works the effects and voices 11-16 out on the second. OFF: one does it all (12 voices at most). ON, OFF; ON at first
TUNING
The pitch of A4. 430-450 Hz, 440 at first

The keys, the knobs and the look

THEME
The screen's colours. DEFAULT, EGA, PASTEL, NEON, SUNSET, ICE, CLEAR, GREEN, AMBER, WHITE, DAYLIGHT, PAPER, MANUAL, SILVER, LCD, COMMANDER; DEFAULT at first
KEYS VOLUME
The volume of the notes the keys play and write (the keys have no velocity). 01-7F, 60 at first
KEYS GAIN
How loud you hear the keys while playing them (the FM-1's and MIDI's), not how they are recorded. -24 to +12 dB, 0 dB at first
KEYS
THROUGH FX: the keys go through the effects as everything else. DRY: past every effect, straight to the output. THROUGH FX, DRY; THROUGH FX at first
KEYS OCTAVE
The keys' octave at power-on. -4 to +4, 0 at first
KNOB SPEED
Bigger steps when a knob is turned fast. OFF, LOW, HIGH; LOW at first
HOLD DELAY
How long a button must be held to count as held. 300-1500 ms, 500 at first
COMBO GRACE
How long a button let go still counts for a combination. 0-250 ms, 100 at first
PLAYBACK SCROLL
PLAYBACK scrolls by ROWS (the playing row stays in the middle) or by PAGES. ROWS, PAGES; ROWS at first

Recording and the arpeggiator

COUNT-IN
Before an EDITOR take: 1 or 2 bars of clicks, none, or KEY (your first note starts the take). LOOPS has its own. OFF, 1 BAR, 2 BARS, KEY; 1 BAR at first
QUANTIZE
Where an EDITOR take puts a note: on the nearest line of 2, 3 or 4 rows, the nearest row, or REAL TIME (the row it falls in, its exact time kept with a note delay). The keys sound exactly as written. 2 ROWS, 3 ROWS, 4 ROWS, ROW, REAL TIME; ROW at first
ARP RHYTHM
The arpeggiator's rhythm and the EDITOR's row preview, a row a step: # plays, _ rests. #, #_, #__, #___, ##_, ###_, ##__, #_##_, and two slow ones; #___ at first
ARP PATTERN
The order the arpeggiator's notes take turns in. UP, DOWN, UP-DOWN, RANDOM, AS PLAYED; UP at first
ARP LATCH
How many notes go on playing after you let go of their keys. OFF, 1-8 NOTES; 4 NOTES at first
POWER-ON SCREEN
The screen the FM-1 starts on. STATUS, LAST, or any screen; STATUS at first

TOOLS and ABOUT

On TOOLS, K1 picks a tool and EDIT runs it:

TOOLS
The line of tools below.
CHECK REFERENCES
Lists the references to things that are missing (a note's instrument gone, a song's pattern deleted): MISSING REFERENCES: 3, or NO MISSING REFERENCES.
CHECK STORAGE
Reads every saved record and checks it is whole (CHECKING STORAGE): STORAGE OK, or a list of problems (STORAGE PROBLEMS: 2). It writes nothing.
CLEAN UP
Finds what nothing uses and asks what goes, as a checklist (CLEAN UP: FREES 1.3K): UNUSED PATTERNS, UNUSED SAMPLES (ticked at first), UNUSED INSTRUMENTS (not: most of a bank is a library, not leftovers). SELECT picks a line, K1 ticks it, OCT+ cleans up (CLEANED UP: 4 DELETED; nothing to do: NOTHING TO CLEAN UP). Built-in things are never listed.
AUTO LEVEL ALL
Measures every sound you have and sets its level, after a question; the built-in cartridges keep theirs.
REVERT ALL
Drops every unsaved change, after a question, as SAVE + OCT- does.
FACTORY RESET
Erases everything you saved or uploaded: songs, patterns, loops, your cartridges and samples, your edits of the built-in instruments, DJ's decks, the settings and the MIDI map. PT/FM itself and its built-in instruments stay, so the FM-1 is as it was after its first install, before the demo disk.

It asks twice. First FACTORY RESET? with ERASES ALL YOU SAVED OR UPLOADED and ONLY THE BUILT-IN INSTRUMENTS STAY: OCT+ answers RESET. Then ERASE IT ALL NOW? with THERE IS NO WAY BACK, and, if you have changes not saved yet, how many go with the rest (3 UNSAVED CHANGES LOST TOO): OCT+ answers ERASE. OCT- (KEEP) or HOME at either question leaves everything as it was.

Then ERASING shows a bar for up to two seconds, the sound silent and every control waiting, and the FM-1 restarts on its power-on screen with the settings as they come.

To keep a copy first, read the FM-1 on the editor page and download a disk (chapter 17). The demo songs come back with the install page's UPLOAD DEMO DISK (chapter 2). A power cut while it erases leaves part of your work: run FACTORY RESET again.

ABOUT
EDIT opens it: the version and the licences. EDIT closes it.

The MIDI page

The second page (SEQ), the same kind of list (chapter 16 explains MIDI in full):

MIDI IN CHANNEL
The channel MIDI in listens to, on USB and the jack. ALL, 1-16; ALL at first
MIDI OUT
What goes out over USB: OFF, KEYS (the FM-1's keys as played) or ALL (the keys and everything that plays: track 0 on channel 1 ... track 15 on channel 16). OFF, KEYS, ALL; KEYS at first
MIDI OUT CHANNEL
The keys' channel out. 1-16; 1 at first
SYNC
INTERNAL: PT/FM's own tempo. EXTERNAL: an outside MIDI clock leads (USB or the jack). INTERNAL, EXTERNAL; INTERNAL at first
CLOCK OUT
Sends the clock, START / STOP / CONTINUE and the song position out, with SYNC INTERNAL. OFF, ON; OFF at first
MIDI CONTROLLER
EDIT turns the FM-1 into a MIDI controller for a computer until it is switched off and on, after a question (chapter 16).
LEARN
How many mappings of 64 (3 OF 64). EDIT learns one: touch an FM-1 control, then move the MIDI control that should drive it (TOUCH AN FM-1 CONTROL, MOVE THE MIDI CONTROL). The mappings are listed below; LFO deletes the one picked.
