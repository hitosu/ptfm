The instruments and the demo disk

What PT/FM brings: the built-in instruments, always there, and the demo disk, which the install page uploads (chapter 2). Cartridges and songs are listed by the numbers they show on the FM-1.

Eleven synth cartridges are part of PT/FM itself, numbered F0 to FA. They are there after every install, after CLEAN INSTALL and after FACTORY RESET. Your edits of them are saved apart, and BACK TO BUILT-IN drops them (chapter 4).

The FUSION cartridge's six instruments are layers of the others (chapter 8); their patterns are E0 to E5.

SPACE PAD
Two pads and a deep bass on every key, a bell from C-4 up.
DUB CHORDS
A minor 9 chord, rolled, with a sub bass, on every key.
SPLIT
A held bass under C-4, a space lead from C-4 up.
CHIPIN
A chip arpeggio on any key in C minor pentatonic, its echo, a triangle bass on C, a blip.
COIN
A game's coin on any key: a square a fourth under the key for a moment, then the key's note ringing out.
DRUM KIT
A drum on every key from F-2 to G-8, made of the synth cartridges' drums and the DRUM KIT and PERCUSSION samplers. The kick is on C-4, with the General MIDI drum kit's order from B-3 to G-5 and hand percussion on the six keys under it; fills, rolls, a flam and a drag on the octave under; the DRUM KIT sampler's drums alone on the octave over (C the kick, D the snare, F# and A# the hats, the toms between); the TB-303's drums above that, then hand drums and chimes. Each drum rings out however short the tap.

The DJ loops

Twelve loops for DJ's decks (chapter 10): each an 8-bar pattern that loops (DNB, ROCK, METAL and CHIP LOOP two: a verse and a bridge). All are in A minor, so any two mix in key (WORLD LOOP's flute aside: its B flat and C sharp go with the others' drums and bass, not their hooks), and at the same loudness. Each track plays one instrument, so LOOPS can load a pattern of them as loops, a loop a track (chapter 14), and every loop keeps the same tracks: 1 the kick, 2 the hats, 3 the bass, 4 the hook, then the rest. TECHNO, ACID, DISCO, DNB, ROCK and METAL LOOP start with an intro of 8 bars, played once, to mix in over the other deck.
