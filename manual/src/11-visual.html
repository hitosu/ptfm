VISUAL

VISUAL shows the sound: each voice's wave, the output as an oscilloscope, a scrolling waveform, a spectrum, a meter for each track, and FOMA, PT/FM's mascot. Open it with HOME + PIT.

The views

SELECT turns the views a step at a time (it stops at both ends); SEQ goes to the next and round. The header names the view (VISUAL SCOPE).

VOICES
A small scope for each voice, labelled with what plays it (T03 a song's track, KEY the keys, L05 a loop) and its note.
SCOPE
The output as an oscilloscope, left above, right below.
WAVE
The output's waveform over the last seconds, scrolling.
SPECTRUM
The output's spectrum: bass on the left, treble on the right (30 Hz to 18 kHz).
LEVELS
A meter for each track, 00 to 0F.
FOMA
PT/FM's mascot: a little FM-1 with a cat's ears, flying into the stars, its rainbow tail waving with the sound.

Zoom, gain and options

  • K1 zooms SCOPE in time, 1 to 6 (K1 ZOOM 3): from 128 to 1,024 samples across the screen.
  • K2 makes every view taller or flatter, x0.13 to x16 in doublings (K2 GAIN X1).
  • GLO opens VISUAL OPTIONS: VIEW STEREO or MONO, and FREEZE OFF or ON, to hold the picture still (the footer then says FROZEN).

VISUAL works from a copy of the sound, never slowing it down.
