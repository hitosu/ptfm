PT/FM at a glance

PT/FM stands for Pocket Tracker / Fusion Machine. It is a complete music workstation in the FM-1: write songs, play them, play over them live, and keep everything on the FM-1 itself.

What it does

  • A tracker. Music is written as patterns: a grid of up to 16 tracks across and up to 128 rows down, each row a step in time. A cell holds a note, the instrument that plays it, a volume and up to 4 commands (a slide, an arpeggio, a tempo change ...). A song plays patterns in an order of your choice. The EDITOR writes patterns; SONGS arranges them.
  • Six kinds of instrument, sharing one pool of voices; INSTRUMENTS picks, tries and edits them:
    • DX7: 6-operator FM, the classic digital synth.
    • JUNO-106: the classic analogue polysynth.
    • TB-303: the acid bass line, with slide and accent.
    • CHIP: the sound chips of 8-bit computers and consoles.
    • Sampler: recorded sounds, with loops and an envelope.
    • FUSION: an instrument made of a pattern, where one note is one pattern row. Each key plays its own row of the pattern (C-4 plays row 3C), every note written in that row at once, each on its own instrument: one key can play a chord, a layered sound, or a different drum on every key, a kit. The row's commands play as well, so a key can also start an arpeggio or a short riff (chapter 8).
  • Up to 16 notes at once (SETTINGS' VOICES: 8, 12 or 16), from the songs, the loops and the keys together.
  • Effects: GATE, FILTER, DRIVE, LO-FI, CHORUS, DELAY, REVERB, LIMITER and DUCK, each song with its own setup, plus a METRONOME. Held on the white keys, sixteen punch-in effects play over everything for as long as a key is down (FX).
  • LOOPS, a loop station: record up to 16 loops of one length on top of each other, mute and solo them from the keys, and record the whole performance as a new song (RECORD LIVE).
  • DJ: two songs on two decks, kept in time, mixed with a crossfader, with filters, pads and an LFO.
  • MIDI: play PT/FM from a keyboard or a computer (USB, or the TRS MIDI IN jack), learn an outside controller's knobs and pads onto the FM-1's own, send the keys and everything that plays out over USB, or make the FM-1 a controller for the computer (MIDI CONTROLLER).
  • Sync with other gear, three ways (chapter 16):
    • MIDI clock in (SETTINGS' SYNC EXTERNAL): PT/FM follows a sequencer's or a drum machine's clock, from USB or the MIDI IN jack, with its START, STOP, CONTINUE and song position, locked to it so nothing drifts.
    • MIDI clock out (CLOCK OUT ON): PT/FM leads, sending the clock, START / STOP / CONTINUE and the song position over USB.
    • Audio sync pulse (SETTINGS' OUTPUT SYNC PULSE L or R): a click on one side of the output every half beat, for instruments that follow a sync pulse, the music in mono on the other side. It works while PT/FM leads (SYNC INTERNAL).
  • USB audio: a computer connected by USB hears PT/FM as a sound card and can record it.
  • Everything stays on the FM-1: SAVE writes your songs, patterns, instruments and settings into its own memory, where they survive power-off and firmware updates.

What comes with it

Built into the firmware are 11 synth cartridges, numbered F0 to FA: eight DX7 cartridges (F0 KEYS/ORGANS to F7 PERC/FX), F8 JUNO-106 (128 sounds), F9 CHIPTUNE (128 sounds) and FA TB-303 (64 sounds). They are always there, even on an empty FM-1. You can edit them like anything else; SAVE keeps your changes, and the originals come back with BACK TO BUILT-IN.

The demo disk is uploaded from the install page (chapter 2). It adds five sampler cartridges (a drum kit, percussion, band and orchestra instruments, voices, basses), the FUSION cartridge, six demo songs and twelve DJ loops. Appendix D lists them all.

The screens

PT/FM has ten screens. Each black key opens one (press HOME, then the black key), and FX has a button of its own:

KeyScreenFor
OP1SONGSyour songs: the list, and each song's order of patterns
OP2EDITORwriting patterns
OP3INSTRUMENTScartridges and instruments: choose, try, edit
OP4PLAYBACKfollowing what plays
OP5DJtwo songs mixed live
PITVISUALthe sound made visible
GLOSTATUSthe start screen: version, help, performance, storage
MONOSETTINGSsettings and tools
POLYLOOPSthe loop station
FXFXeffects, punch-ins, the metronome, the mixer

The black key OP6 and the blank key have no screen yet.
