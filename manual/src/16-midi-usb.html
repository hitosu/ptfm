MIDI and USB

Connected by USB, the FM-1 is a sound card and a MIDI device for a computer or a phone, with nothing to install. The TRS MIDI IN jack takes a keyboard. SETTINGS' MIDI page (chapter 13) sets it all.

Playing PT/FM from MIDI

MIDI comes in over USB and through the MIDI IN jack (a TRS-to-DIN adapter, type A or B, for a keyboard's DIN cable); both behave the same.

  • Notes play as the FM-1's keys do, on every screen: they play the current instrument, write in the EDITOR with REC on, record into LOOPS, feed the arpeggiator. Velocity sets the note's volume. Every note plays, not only the keys' range.
  • MIDI IN CHANNEL picks the channel listened to (ALL at first).
  • Program change picks the instrument in the current cartridge, as PRESETS does: program n is the cartridge's n-th sound, counted from 0 and skipping empty slots, so it is not always slot n. Bank select (CC 0) picks the cartridge, as SEL + PRESETS does: bank n is the n-th cartridge in the cartridge list, counted from 0, not cartridge number n. With the demo disk, bank 0 is 01 DRUM KIT and bank 6 is F0.
  • Pitch bend bends the keys' notes, plus or minus 2 semitones. The sustain pedal (CC 64) holds the notes played from MIDI.
  • Every other controller does nothing until you learn it onto an FM-1 control (below).

MIDI learn

An outside controller's knobs and pads can work the FM-1's own controls: its 7 knobs, MASTER, every button but HOME (HOME cancels learning, so it cannot be learned) and the 27 keys. A learned control works exactly as the FM-1's, on every screen, with its combinations and holds.

  1. SETTINGS, the MIDI page (SEQ), turn SELECT to LEARN and press EDIT.
  2. TOUCH AN FM-1 CONTROL: turn the knob or press the button or key to learn (it does nothing else then).
  3. MOVE THE MIDI CONTROL: turn the outside knob, or press and let go the pad. The FM-1 works out what kind it is (an absolute knob, an endless encoder, a momentary or a toggle pad) and says what it learned (K1: CC 74, CH 1).

HOME cancels at either step. Up to 64 mappings; the list below LEARN shows them, and LFO deletes the one picked. SAVE keeps them.

MIDI out and the clock

  • MIDI OUT goes over USB only (the FM-1 has no MIDI OUT jack): OFF, KEYS (the FM-1's keys as played, on MIDI OUT CHANNEL) or ALL (also everything that plays: track 0 on channel 1 ... track 15 on channel 16). Notes that came in over MIDI are never sent back.
  • SYNC EXTERNAL follows an outside MIDI clock, from USB or the jack: its tempo, START, STOP, CONTINUE and the song position (a sequencer can start PT/FM from any bar). PT/FM locks to the clock itself, not only its tempo, so nothing drifts. In LOOPS the clock drives the loops; on DJ, the main deck. Lost for half a second, PT/FM plays on at the last tempo (CLOCK LOST).
  • CLOCK OUT ON sends the clock, START / STOP / CONTINUE and the song position, timed to the sound as heard.
  • SYNC PULSE: for instruments that follow a sync pulse through an audio cable, set SETTINGS' OUTPUT to SYNC PULSE L or R and use a stereo-split cable: a click on that side every half beat, the music in mono on the other. MASTER sets the clicks' level too. It works with SYNC INTERNAL only: while PT/FM follows a clock (SYNC EXTERNAL), the output stays stereo and sends no clicks.

USB audio

  • The FM-1 is a class-compliant USB audio input: no driver on Windows, macOS, Linux or a phone. Record it like any microphone or interface.
  • 16-bit stereo at 44.1 or 48 kHz, whichever the computer picks, kept in step with the computer's clock, so a recording never drifts or clicks.
  • It carries the mix after the effects, always stereo, at SETTINGS' USB GAIN (MASTER does not touch it). The metronome and the count-in's clicks stay off it.
  • No audio goes back from the computer to the FM-1.

MIDI CONTROLLER

The FM-1 can become a USB-MIDI controller for a computer: its keys, knobs and buttons send MIDI to a synth or a music program, and its screen shows what it sends.

  1. SETTINGS, the MIDI page, SELECT to MIDI CONTROLLER, EDIT. It asks first (SWITCH TO MIDI CONTROLLER?, PT/FM STOPS UNTIL THE FM-1 IS SWITCHED OFF AND ON), then whether to save unsaved changes (SAVE BEFORE SWITCHING?).
  2. PT/FM stops. The screen asks for the mode: REC for MIRROR (channel 16), HOME for GENERAL (channel 1); SELECT first picks another channel.
  3. Play. Switch the FM-1 off and on to get PT/FM back.
GENERAL
A keyboard controller: the keys send notes (OCT- and OCT+ shift them, -3 to +3, both together back to 0); K1-K4 send CC 74, 71, 73, 72 (cutoff, resonance, attack, release); SELECT CC 1 (modulation); ALGORITHM CC 21; PRESETS program change; MASTER CC 7 (volume); PLAY starts and stops a music program (MIDI Machine Control, and CC 114); the other buttons CC 102 and up.
MIRROR
Every control sends one fixed message, for working the virtual FM-1 of PT/FM's web pages from the real panel; the page lights the FM-1's LEDs back.

Nothing is saved in controller mode, and nothing is written to the FM-1's memory.
