LOOPS

LOOPS is a loop station: you record a layer in one pass, then the next over it, without stopping the music. You keep a stack of up to 16 loops, mute and solo them while playing over them, and can record the whole performance as a new song. Open it with HOME + POLY.

The loops

  • Up to 16 loops, listed in the order they were made, the newest at the bottom, with + NEW LOOP under them. A loop's number is its place, 00 to 0F.
  • One length for all, set by the first loop in whole bars (8 bars at most at 1/16). After that only the properties' LENGTH and the menu's DOUBLE change it.
  • A loop has one instrument and a colour of its own. PRESETS (and ENV, the cartridges) pick the sound the keys play and the next loop takes, never a recorded loop's.
  • POLY or MONO, chosen before recording (the black keys MONO and POLY in CONTROL mode; POLY at first): a POLY loop holds up to 4 notes at once, a MONO loop one, a note over a held one taking over as the properties' MONO LEGATO says.
  • A loop's mix: K1 to K4 turn the selected loop's LEVEL, PAN, DELAY SEND and REVERB SEND (CHORUS and DUCK on FX's MIXER). On + NEW LOOP they set the next loop's mix.
  • ALGORITHM turns the tempo, any time.
  • LOOPS has its own effects (FX from LOOPS edits them) and metronome, so it sounds the same whatever song is current. SAVE keeps the loops like any change.
What plays (a song, a pattern) plays on into LOOPS until the loops start. The loops play on outside LOOPS until a song or a pattern starts. Leaving LOOPS for any screen but FX ends a take.

Recording a loop

  1. Pick a sound with PRESETS. Turn SELECT to + NEW LOOP (for a new loop) or to a loop (to record it again).
  2. Press REC: the take is armed.
    • With the loops playing, it starts at their next start; the row counts down (ARMED: STARTS IN 3 BARS).
    • With nothing playing, your first note starts the loops and the take at once; or press PLAY to start them after the properties' COUNT-IN.
  3. Play. A take is one pass of the length and stops by itself, the loops playing on. Press REC to stop it early; REC while armed cancels (CANCELLED).

The first loop has no length to fill: it grows a bar at a time while you play. Press REC to close it on a bar line (LOOP 00: 4 BARS); its bars are the length. Played nothing: NOTHING PLAYED: NO LOOP. PLAY with no loops, nothing armed and nothing playing is refused: REC, THEN PLAY: THE FIRST LOOP.

  • What you hear is what is kept. While recording, notes land on QUANTIZE's grid and sound there; a MONO loop plays one note at a time, a POLY one at most 4. Not recording, the keys play freely.
  • RE-RECORD: EDIT (or REC on a loop) records the selected loop again; its old notes are gone the moment the take is armed (LOOP 03 RE-RECORDING). It keeps its place, colour, instrument and mix.
  • PEEL: LFO removes the selected loop (on + NEW LOOP, the last) without asking (LOOP 03 PEELED); SEL's REVERT brings back what was saved.
  • A full edit memory ends a take, keeping what was written (MEMORY FOR EDITS IS FULL).

QUANTIZE

Where a recorded note lands: the properties' QUANTIZE, or the black keys OP1-OP5 in CONTROL mode (QUANTIZE: 2 ROWS).

2 ROWS, 3 ROWS, 4 ROWS
The nearest line of that many rows, from the loops' start.
ROW
The nearest row (at first).
REAL TIME
The row it falls in, its exact time kept as a note delay, its end as a key off.

A note played a little early waits for its line; one just after it sounds at once and is written on the line.

CONTROL mode: mutes and solos

In CONTROL mode the keys stop playing notes and become a mixer for the loops. Each white key is a loop: white key 1 is the top loop, white key 16 the sixteenth.

  1. Press SEQ. The FM-1 shows KEYS: CONTROL and the header reads SEQ MUTE.
  2. Tap a white key: its loop mutes (LOOP 02 MUTED) or plays again (LOOP 02 ON) at once.
  3. Press SEQ again to play notes: KEYS: PLAY.

Hold a white key a moment instead (longer than HOLD DELAY in SETTINGS) and the change waits for the next bar line (LOOP 02 MUTED AT THE BAR); the loop's row shows a yellow M while it waits.

Press the black key GLO to make the white keys solo instead of mute (SEQ: SOLO; again: SEQ: MUTE). One loop is soloed at a time: a white key solos its loop alone (LOOP 02 SOLO), and on the soloed loop it ends the solo (LOOP 02 SOLO OFF). Held, the solo waits for the bar line (a yellow S).

While a loop is soloed only it sounds, but the mutes are kept and come back when the solo ends. SAVE keeps the mutes with the loops; a solo is never saved, so after a power-on no loop is soloed.

The pattern recorder

ARP opens a step sequencer over the loops, which play on, so the pattern is heard in the mix while you build it. (The EDITOR's SEL, RECORD PATTERN is the same recorder.)

  • 4 rows of 16 steps, a note a row (with a kit, each row a drum). SELECT picks the row; a white key switches its step on or off.
  • The row's note: K3 moves it a semitone a click; or tap SEL, then press a key: that key is the note (KEYS: THE NOTE, then KEYS: THE STEPS). SEL held + a key does it at once. OCT- / OCT+ move the row's note with the octave (NOTE C-5).
  • K1 STEP SIZE (1/32 to 1/4, 1/16 at first), K2 LENGTH (4, 8, 12 or 16 steps), K4 TIMES (x1-x8, for the first loop only: how many times it fills the new length).
  • GLO: the notes RING TO THE NEXT (at first) or last A STEP LONG (NOTES RING TO THE NEXT, NOTES A STEP LONG).
  • The preview plays while the recorder is open; ARP switches it off and on.
  • REC writes it and closes: on + NEW LOOP a new loop (PATTERN: LOOP 06, 4 BARS); opened on a loop, it replaces that loop's notes (PATTERN INTO LOOP 03). No step on: NO STEPS ON. HOME closes it, nothing written.

What you set stays for the next time until power-off.

RECORD LIVE: the performance as a song

SEL, RECORD LIVE records everything you do over the loops into a new song: the loops as they play, mutes and solos, the keys, effects, punch-ins, tempo changes and the mix.

  1. SEL, RECORD LIVE opens its panel: the song's NAME (EDIT types it) and K1 TEMPO.
  2. REC arms it (REC blinks; the top line's REC reads SONG). HOME cancels.
  3. It starts with your next REC, a note, or PLAY.
  4. Perform. PLAY drops the loops out and brings them back; PLAY held takes them to their start; the keys play on their own tracks.
  5. REC ends it (SONG 12 RECORDED: 1:23, 5 PATTERNS). SAVE keeps the song.
  • While a song records, nothing changes the loops themselves: takes, PEEL, DOUBLE and the like are refused (RECORDING A SONG).
  • Leaving LOOPS (but for FX) ends it, keeping what was written.
  • Notes that do not fit the song's 16 tracks are left out and counted (SONG 12: 3 NOTES LEFT OUT). It stops by itself when the memory, the pattern numbers or 256 positions run out (SONG 12 STOPPED: MEMORY FOR EDITS IS FULL); with no song number free it is refused (NO SONG NUMBER FREE).

Loops to a pattern and back

LOOPS has no precise editing; for that, store the loops in a pattern and edit it in the EDITOR.

SAVE TO PATTERN
Writes one pattern of the loops' length: every loop's notes, repeats written out, each with its instrument, and each loop's mix (LOOPS STORED IN PATTERN 2A). Pick NEW or a pattern; a pattern that is not empty asks first.
LOAD FROM PATTERN
Turns a pattern into loops, a loop an instrument, replacing the loops after a question (PATTERN 2A IN LOOPS: 3 LOOPS). The length is the pattern's, in whole bars; what does not fit 16 loops of 4 notes is left out and said.

The properties and the menu

GLO opens LOOPS PROPERTIES:

TEMPO
As a song's, with its BPM (ALGORITHM turns it too). 3-600
SWING
50-75 %
LENGTH
A bar more or less: longer adds silence at the end, shorter cuts bars off (asking first when notes would go). Before the first loop: THE FIRST LOOP SETS IT.
GRID
1/16, 1/16T or 1/32: 4, 6 or 8 rows a beat. Only before the first loop (GRID: ONLY BEFORE THE FIRST LOOP).
QUANTIZE
Where a recorded note lands (above): on every 2, 3 or 4 rows, on the nearest row, or where you played it (REAL TIME). 2 ROWS, 3 ROWS, 4 ROWS, ROW, REAL TIME; ROW at first
MONO LEGATO
On a MONO loop, a note over a held one: GLIDE (a short glide; a TB-303's slide), LEGATO (the held note moves to the new one, no new attack) or JUMP (a plain new note).
COUNT-IN
Before a take PLAY starts: OFF, 1 BAR, 2 BARS. A key always starts at once. OFF at first

SEL opens the menu:

APPLY INSTRUMENT
The selected loop takes the current instrument (LOOP 03: 01:04 BRASS).
DOUBLE
The length x2, every loop playing twice what it played (DOUBLED: 8 BARS; past 8 bars: DOUBLE: AT MOST 8 BARS).
RECORD LIVE
Its panel (above); while armed, CANCEL RECORD LIVE.
SAVE TO PATTERN, LOAD FROM PATTERN
Above.
CLEAR LOOPS
Every loop goes, after a question; the next loop sets a new length (LOOPS CLEARED).
REVERT
LOOPS as last saved, after a question.

With no loops, EDIT, LFO and the menu's loop items say NO LOOPS YET.
