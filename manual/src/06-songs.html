SONGS

SONGS lists every song, and below the list shows the picked song's order: the patterns it plays, one after another. The song at the cursor is the current song everywhere: PLAY plays it, and the EDITOR opens on its patterns. Open it with HOME + OP1.

The list

  • SELECT picks a song: it becomes the current song.
  • PLAY plays it, from the boxed position; again, pauses. The playing song has a play mark and its name turns green.
  • ALGORITHM moves the box: the position PLAY will start from (PLAY FROM POS 03 in the header). While the song plays, only the box moves; it waits for the next PLAY.
  • The header counts the songs (3/18); the footer's right side shows the current song's BPM and length.
  • A position whose pattern is missing shows its number in red.

When a song ends: the play modes

SEQ turns the play mode round (ONE PLAY, REPEAT SONG, PLAY NEXT). It decides how a song started here ends, if the song itself stops at its end:

  • ONE PLAY (at first): it stops.
  • REPEAT SONG: it plays again from its start.
  • PLAY NEXT: the next song in the list plays at once, and the cursor follows it; after the last song, playing stops.

A song set to loop (AT THE END: LOOP) loops in every mode. The mode holds on every screen until something else plays (a pattern in the EDITOR, LOOPS, DJ), and it is not saved.

Changing a song's order

Press EDIT on a song: the cursor moves into its order, onto the boxed position, and EDIT lights.

  • SELECT moves a line (8 positions) up or down, ALGORITHM one position left or right.
  • K1 puts another pattern at the position (K1 PATTERN 0A); with SEL held, 8 at a time.
  • LFO removes the position.
  • ENV opens the pattern list; ENV again puts the highlighted pattern at the position.
  • SEL opens the position's menu (below).
  • EDIT leaves the order keeping the changes; HOME leaves dropping them (CHANGES DROPPED): a pattern made meanwhile is deleted again.
EDIT PATTERN
Opens the position's pattern in the EDITOR; HOME comes back here.
NEW PATTERN
An empty pattern at the first free number, put in after the position (PATTERN 0C MADE).
INSERT
A position before the cursor's, with the same pattern.
DELETE
Removes the position, as LFO does.
COPY, PASTE
COPY takes the position's pattern number (PATTERN 03 COPIED); PASTE puts it in after the position.
CLONE PATTERN
Copies the position's pattern to a new number and uses the copy here (03 CLONED TO 0C), so you can change it without changing the other places that play the original.
REVERT
The song as last saved (listed only while it has unsaved changes).

Your patterns are numbered 00 to EF (240 of them). With all used, NEW PATTERN and CLONE PATTERN say NO FREE PATTERN.

The song menu

SEL on the list opens the song's menu:

NEW
A new song. First pick the pattern it starts with from the list (NEW SONG STARTS WITH), or + NEW PATTERN for an empty one; HOME makes nothing. The song, NEW SONG, is added at the end of the list (tempo 120, LOOP). Past song FD: NO FREE SONG NUMBER.
CLONE
A copy of the song, after it, named with " 2".
MOVE
Another place in the list: SELECT carries it, SEL puts it down (SONG MOVED TO 02), HOME takes it back (NOT MOVED).
DELETE
Asks first (DELETE SONG 00?). The line ITS PATTERNS TOO is ticked at first: the song's own patterns, those no other song and no FUSION instrument uses, are deleted with it (SONG 00 DELETED, 4 PATTERNS TOO), and the line says how many there are and how much room they free. To keep them, clear the tick with K1. Patterns other songs use always stay. OCT+ deletes, OCT- keeps the song, HOME cancels.
RENAME
Type its name (up to 16 characters); EDIT is done.
UNLOAD SONG
Once a song, the EDITOR's pattern loop, the loops or DJ's decks have played, the keys go on sounding through that one's effects and tempo, even after it stops (chapter 15). UNLOAD SONG stops everything at once, forgets the song's place, and gives the keys back their own effects and tempo, THE KEYS', as at power-on (SONG UNLOADED). The loops and DJ's stand stay as they are. It is listed only when there is something to unload; PLAY afterwards plays the current song from its start.
REVERT
The song as last saved: its order, name, properties, mixer and effects (not its patterns), after a question (REVERT SONG 00 TO SAVED?).

ENV on the list opens the pattern list; the pattern picked becomes the EDITOR's (PATTERN 0A FOR THE EDITOR); PLAY in the list hears the highlighted pattern.

A song's properties

GLO opens the song's properties. SELECT picks one, K1 changes it at once (SEL held: TEMPO by 10, SWING by 5), EDIT on NAME types it; GLO or HOME closes.

How time works in a song: each row lasts a number of ticks (TICKS A ROW), and TEMPO sets how fast the ticks go (a tick lasts 2.5 seconds divided by TEMPO). Leave TICKS A ROW at 6 and ROWS A BEAT at 4, as a new song has them, and TEMPO is the BPM: for 128 BPM, set TEMPO to 80 (hex for 128). Whatever the three are, the screen shows the BPM they make.

NAME
Up to 16 characters.
TEMPO
How fast the song plays, with the BPM it makes: 8C (140 BPM). 3-600 (hex 03-258)
TICKS A ROW
The ticks in a row: commands that act during a row (slides, arpeggios, retriggers) act on each tick. 1-31
ROWS A BEAT
How many rows make a beat. Besides the BPM, it decides which row numbers the EDITOR colours as beats and bars, and how many rows DJ counts as a beat. 1-16
SWING
Every second row played late. 50-75 %
AT THE END
What the song does after its last position. LOOP, STOP
LOOP FROM
The position a looping song goes back to. POSITION 00 to the last

The BPM is TEMPO × 24 / (TICKS A ROW × ROWS A BEAT), rounded: a song at TEMPO 140, 6 ticks a row, 4 rows a beat plays at 140 BPM.
