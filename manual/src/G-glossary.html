Glossary

The words this manual uses, each in a line, with where it is explained.

The words

BPM
Beats a minute: how fast a song goes. The screens show it beside TEMPO (chapter 6).
Built-in
What PT/FM itself carries: the eleven synth cartridges F0 to FA. They are there after every install; your changes to them are kept apart, and BACK TO BUILT-IN drops them (chapter 4).
Cartridge
A bank of instruments of one kind (DX7, JUNO-106, TB-303, CHIP, sampler or FUSION), numbered 00 to FF. SEL + PRESETS picks one (chapter 8).
Cell
One track's place on one row: a note, its instrument, its volume and up to four commands (chapter 7).
Command
An instruction in a cell that changes how a note plays (a slide, a vibrato, a volume) or how the song goes (a jump, a tempo), written as a letter or sign and two digits, A04 (chapter 7).
Count-in
One or two bars of clicks before a take starts (chapter 7).
Cue point
Where a DJ deck starts from and goes back to (chapter 10).
Current instrument
The instrument the keys play, shown at the footer's left: PRESETS picks it (chapter 4).
Current song
The song at SONGS' cursor: PLAY plays it, and the EDITOR's loop plays at its tempo (chapter 6).
Deck
One of DJ's two players, each with a song of its own (chapter 10).
Edit memory
Where every change waits until SAVE writes it; switching off loses it (chapter 4).
FUSION
An instrument made of a pattern: each key plays one row of it, every note of that row at once (chapter 8).
FX setup
A song's effects: the chain's order, each effect's knobs and on/off, the mixer and the metronome. LOOPS, DJ and THE KEYS have their own (chapter 15).
Hex
Counting in sixteens, the way trackers number things: 00 to 09, then 0A to 0F, then 10; 7F is 127 (the conventions).
Instrument
A sound in a cartridge's slot, written 01:05 (cartridge 01, slot 05) (chapter 8).
Loop
On LOOPS, a recorded line of notes that repeats; up to 16 play together (chapter 14).
Order
The list of patterns a song plays, one after another (chapter 6).
Pattern
A grid of notes: up to 16 tracks across and 128 rows down, played from top to bottom. Songs are made of patterns (chapter 7).
Position
A place in a song's order, numbered from 00; each position plays one pattern (chapter 6).
Punch-in
An effect played live while a white key is held with FX: a stutter, a tape stop, a filter sweep (chapter 15).
Row
One step in time of a pattern, numbered in hex from 00. With 4 rows a beat, a row is a sixteenth note (chapter 6).
Sample
A recorded sound, kept in a sampler cartridge. An instrument plays it (chapter 8, chapter 17).
Slot
A cartridge's place for one instrument, numbered from 00 (chapter 8).
Song
An order of patterns with its own tempo, mixer and effects (chapter 6).
Take
A live recording in the EDITOR: the pattern plays round and round while what you play is written (chapter 7).
THE KEYS
The keys' own effects and tempo, used before anything has played and after UNLOAD SONG (chapter 15).
Tick
A row's smaller step: a row lasts TICKS A ROW ticks (6 at first), and commands such as slides act on every tick (chapter 6).
Toast
A short message in the header, for two seconds (chapter 3).
Track
A column of a pattern, numbered 00 to 0F; each plays one line of notes, and HOME's combination mutes it (chapter 4).
User area
The FM-1's memory for what you save and upload. An install never writes it (chapter 2).
Voice
One sounding note. PT/FM plays up to 16 at once (SETTINGS' VOICES, chapter 13).
