DJ

DJ turns the FM-1 into a DJ stand: two songs on two decks, kept in time, mixed with a crossfader, with a filter, loops, cue points and stops on each deck. It is only for performing: nothing done on DJ changes a song. Open it with HOME + OP5.

The stand

  • The left deck is the main: its tempo is the set's tempo, and the right deck can follow it (SYNC).
  • Each deck shows its song, its BPM and the change from the song's own tempo, its position and bar, an overview of the song with the time left, its filter, and its loop or repeat.
  • The crossfader (ALGORITHM) blends the two: all the way left, only the main is heard (as at first).
  • The song list below: SELECT moves its highlight; L, R or LR mark the songs on the decks.
DJ stays apart until it plays. Opening DJ changes nothing of what plays: you can set up the decks in silence and leave. DJ's first start (a deck's PLAY key, say) stops what was playing and cuts its sound, so the decks start dry. PLAY on DJ while something else plays first pauses it (PAUSED: PLAY AGAIN FOR DJ); the next PLAY plays the decks. Once DJ plays, the decks play on outside DJ until a song, a pattern or the loops start.

Loading and playing

  1. Turn SELECT to a song in the list.
  2. Press OP4 to load it on the left deck, or OP5 on the right (LEFT: CITY LIGHTS). A song with no positions is refused (SONG 0A IS EMPTY).
  3. Press OP1 to play or pause the left deck, OP6 the right. Held, the deck goes back to its song's start, paused (LEFT: BACK TO THE START).
  4. PLAY plays or pauses both decks at once; held, both go back to their cue points (BACK TO THE CUES).
  • Loading onto a playing deck waits for its next bar line (LEFT: CITY LIGHTS AT THE BAR; the LOAD key blinks): the new song takes over at the bar, at the BPM the deck was playing, so the beat goes on. Hold the LOAD key instead to have it start at its own tempo (LEFT: CITY LIGHTS AT ITS TEMPO). Tap LOAD again before the bar line to cancel (LEFT: LOAD CANCELLED).
  • The same song can be on both decks, to fade from one part of it to another.
  • At a song's end it loops or stops, as the song says. Stopped, its PLAY has nothing more to play (THE END: CUE GOES BACK). Through the last 8 bars of a song that stops, the time left turns red and the deck's PLAY key blinks on the beat.
  • An empty deck refuses PLAY, CUE, SYNC, HOLD and FILTER: NO SONG ON THE DECK.

SWAP: SEQ exchanges the decks (DECKS SWAPPED; both empty: NO SONGS TO SWAP): songs, places, cue points, tempos, filters, loops and mutes. Nothing heard changes. The usual flow: the main plays; you load the next song on the right, start it in time and bring it in with the crossfader; SWAP; the right deck is free for the next one.

Tempo and SYNC

  • K1 is the left deck's tempo (the set's), K4 the right's: shown as the BPM it plays and the change (TEMPO L 124.0 BPM +3.2%), 3 to 600 BPM in steps of 0.1 (SEL held: 1 BPM). The song's own tempo commands still act, scaled. SEL after a turn sets it back to the song's own (TEMPO 120.0 BPM: THE SONG'S).
  • MONO is SYNC (on at first): the right deck follows the main's tempo (or its half or double, whichever is nearest its own song's) and its beats fall on the main's. A synced right deck starts on the main's next bar line; its tempo knob then does nothing (SYNC: TEMPO ON THE MAIN DECK). SYNC ON, SYNC OFF.
  • With SETTINGS' SYNC set to EXTERNAL, an outside MIDI clock leads the main deck (SYNC EXTERNAL: THE CLOCK'S TEMPO); START plays it from its cue point, STOP pauses it.
  • Two decks at one BPM never drift apart.

Filters, volumes and effects

  • K2 is the left deck's DJ FILTER, K3 the right's: one way a low-pass (LP, darker), the other a high-pass (HP, thinner), OFF in the middle (FILTER L LP 2.4K).
  • OP3 (the left) and the black key GLO (the right) switch the filter off and back on, sliding there while the key is held (FILTER L OFF, FILTER L ON LP 2.4K). With the filter off, the knob sets the filter silently, ready for the key to bring it in on the beat.
  • ENV switches K2 and K3 to the decks' VOLUME and back (K2 K3: VOLUME, K2 K3: FILTER): +6 dB down to -24 dB and OFF, a dB a click (VOLUME L -3 DB).
  • SEL tapped puts the last knob turned back: a tempo to the song's own, a filter to OFF, the crossfader to the middle, a volume to 0 dB, a KEY to 0.
  • Each deck plays its song's mixer and its own GATE, FILTER, DRIVE, LO-FI and DUCK. CHORUS, DELAY and REVERB are DJ's own, shared by both decks, with DJ's settings: FX on DJ edits them, with the LIMITER and the METRONOME, and FX again comes back to DJ.
  • FX + white key held plays a punch-in over the mix, as everywhere.
  • When all 16 voices are busy, the deck the crossfader favours less gives way.

A hand on the record: HOLD

OP2 is the left deck's HOLD, PIT the right's:

  • Held down, the deck stops where it is, as a hand on the record.
  • Turn the deck's tempo knob meanwhile (K1 left, K4 right): it moves by hand, a row a click, heard. Its filter knob (K2, K3) moves it silently, a beat a click, for the long way.
  • Let go: a deck that was playing plays on from there at once. So a tap holds a deck back by that much, to nudge it against the other.

The pads

The white keys are each deck's pads: keys 1-8 the left deck's, 9-16 the right's. The BLANK key turns their mode between PLAY pads and LOOP pads (PADS: PLAY, PADS: LOOPS); LFO brings in the LFO pads.

Pad (left, right)PLAY pads
1 9SET CUE: sets the cue point on the bar line nearest the tap, while the deck plays on (CUE: POS 02 BAR 4). Held a quarter second, on the beat instead.
2 10CUE: as a DJ player's CUE. Paused, sets the cue point. Playing, plays from the cue point while held, and pauses there when let go. Press the deck's PLAY key while CUE is held, and it plays on when you let go.
3 11JUMP: back to the cue point, playing on; held, the jump lands on the next beat.
4 12MOVE: held, the deck's knobs move a cursor: K1 (the left deck's; K4 the right's) a bar a click, and K2 (K3) a pattern a click (MOVE L POS 03 BAR 2); let go, the deck moves there on the next beat, in phase, playing on.
5 13BRAKE: held, the deck slows and its pitch falls, a turntable's stop.
6 14BACKSPIN: held, the record spun back by hand.
7 15ECHO OUT: held, the deck's sound goes into the delay and stops on the next beat, the echoes ringing on.
8 16REVERB OUT: the same into the reverb.

The stops (pads 5-8) work while held: let go, the deck plays on where it would have been, in time, as if it had never stopped; a tap is a dip. Press the deck's PLAY key while a stop is held and the deck stays stopped when you let go.

A loop stays through a pause; a pad that cannot act (a stop on a paused deck, say) blinks fast.

The LFO pads

LFO brings in the LFO pads (PADS: LFO), and LFO again goes back. They make things swing by themselves, each at a note length of the tempo (1/2 at first). Hold LFO to see which pad does what.

Mutes, the CUT, saving

  • HOME + white key mutes a deck's track: keys 1-8 the left deck's tracks 00-07, keys 9-16 the right deck's (LEFT TRACK 02 MUTED). SEQ first, or the key held: solo (LEFT TRACK 02 SOLO).
  • HOME + OCT+ takes those keys to tracks 08-0F (MUTES: TRACKS 08-0F); HOME + OCT- back (MUTES: TRACKS 00-07). Alone, OCT does nothing on DJ.
  • HOME + PLAY takes both decks back to their cue points; HOME + REC cuts the sound while the decks play on.
  • SAVE keeps the whole stand (the decks as they stand, the crossfader, SYNC, the LFOs): at power-on the decks are back, paused, and PLAY goes on from the same point.

DJ's properties

GLO opens DJ PROPERTIES:

CROSSFADER
SMOOTH (each deck at -3 dB in the middle, as loud through the blend) or SHARP (both full until the last 2 clicks at either end, for cuts).
FILTER RESONANCE
The DJ filters' resonance. 0-100 %
FILTER THE SENDS
ON: a deck's filter also filters what it sends to the shared effects, so a sweep takes its echoes along. OFF saves some processor time. ON, OFF
FX LFO
What the FX LFO swings. REVERB MIX, DELAY MIX, CHORUS MIX
RESET DJ
EDIT, after a question (RESET DJ?, BOTH DECKS EMPTIED, ALL AS AT FIRST): both decks emptied, everything as at first (DJ RESET).
