Specifications

PT/FM on the FM-1, in figures.

Sound

EnginesDX7 (6-operator FM, 32 algorithms), JUNO-106, TB-303, CHIP (8-bit and 16-bit era sound chips), sampler, FUSION (layers of the others, played from a pattern)
Voices16 at most (SETTINGS' VOICES: 8, 12 or 16), shared by the songs, the loops, DJ's decks and the keys; 16 with the second processor running, else 12
InstrumentsCartridges of up to 32 (DX7), 128 (JUNO-106, TB-303, CHIP) or 64 (sampler, FUSION) instruments; 11 built in (F0-FA, 568 instruments), yours numbered 00-EF
Samples8-bit or 16-bit, stored losslessly compressed; up to 64 a sampler cartridge
EffectsGATE, FILTER, DRIVE, LO-FI, CHORUS, DELAY, REVERB, LIMITER, DUCK and the METRONOME, in a chain you order; 16 punch-in effects; a mixer of 16 tracks with three sends
Sample rate44.1 kHz
TuningA4 = 430-450 Hz

Songs, patterns and loops

PatternsUp to 240 (00-EF), each of 1-16 tracks and 1-128 rows
SongsUp to 254 (00-FD), each of 1-256 positions
LoopsUp to 16 at once in LOOPS
DJTwo decks, each a song
TempoTEMPO 3-600 (at 6 ticks a row and 4 rows a beat, the BPM)
Storage412 KB of flash for your work and uploads (a part kept free for saving); unsaved edits in working memory until SAVE

Connections and controls

USBMIDI in and out, class compliant; audio to the computer (UAC1, 16-bit stereo, 44.1 or 48 kHz); installs and uploads from a Chrome or Edge page
MIDI INTRS jack (a TRS-to-DIN adapter, type A or B, for a DIN cable)
OutputStereo output jack (a cable in it mutes the speaker); the built-in speaker (one channel)
Display240 x 240 colour LCD
Controls27 keys (F to G, two octaves and a bit), 14 buttons, 7 endless knobs, MASTER
FirmwarePT/FM, installed over the FM-1's own update path; USB RESCUE (OCT- held at power-on) always there
