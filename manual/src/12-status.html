STATUS

STATUS is the start screen: the FM-1 opens on it (unless SETTINGS' POWER-ON SCREEN says otherwise). It shows PT/FM's version and how to get help, and the figures behind the header: how busy the sound keeps the processor, the voices, the storage and memory, the device. Open it with HOME + GLO.

The pages

SELECT or ALGORITHM turns the pages:

PT/FM
The name, the version, PRESS HOME + LFO FOR HELP, and the figures a glance needs:
CPU 1, MAXHow much of its time the first processor spends on the sound now, and the most since the last reset. Green up to 50 %, yellow to 80 %, red above.
CPU 2The same for the second processor (effects and voices 11-16); OFF while it does not run.
HALFThe longest piece of sound work, in microseconds: over 5804 is too slow.
LATEPieces of sound not ready in time: each one is a click (dropout).
STEALSNotes that gave their voice up to a new note because all were busy.
VOICES, MAXVoices sounding now and the limit (01/12), and the most at once.
EDITS FREEThe memory left for unsaved edits.
FLASH FREEThe free storage, where saves go.
UNSAVEDHow many things changed since the last SAVE.
BATTERY, UPThe battery, and the time since power-on.
SONGThe song playing, else the current song.
PERFORMANCE
Each processor's load now, on average and at most; the worst sound piece, late pieces and steals; each engine's share; voices; the effects' cost; a graph of the last 30 seconds.
STORAGE
The user area (where everything you keep goes), used and free; what it holds by kind; unsaved changes and the memory they take.
USER FLASH MAP
The user area as a map, a square a kilobyte, each in the colour of what it holds.
RAM MAP
The FM-1's 512 KB of memory as a map, a square a kilobyte.
DEVICE
The battery, the firmware version, the output (stereo, mono, sync pulse), MASTER, the screen's frames a second, the time since power-on, VOICES, THEME and the second processor's state. SECOND CORE reads STARTING in the first two seconds, then ON; OFF when SETTINGS' SECOND CORE is off; or, in red, why it stopped until the next start: STOPPED: NO ANSWER, STOPPED: MISREAD, STOPPED: STUCK, STOPPED: FAULT, or STOPPED: AFTER A CRASH (after PT/FM crashed, the second processor stays off until the FM-1 is switched off and on). Without it, the FM-1 plays on with 12 voices at most. BATTERY reads 1, 2 or 3 bars, or USB POWER.

The footer's right side shows the free storage (FREE 237K).

Keys on STATUS

KeyDoes
SELECT or ALGORITHMTurns the pages, one a click.
PLAYPauses and resumes what played last; in the song list, plays the song highlighted.
LFOZeroes MAX, HALF, LATE and STEALS (COUNTS ZEROED).
ENVOpens the song list: PLAY there plays the highlighted song from its start and goes to SONGS.
EDITGoes to SETTINGS.
HOME + LFOThe help: on STATUS it opens on the keys every screen shares.
ARPSwitches the arpeggiator on and off.
ARP heldWith SELECT, ALGORITHM or K1: the arpeggiator's rhythm, order and latch.
