AUTOMATIONS

AUTOMATIONS records what you do on the FM-1, every key, button and knob at the times you worked them, and plays it back: a performance of mutes, knob sweeps or notes played into LOOPS, or a piece of work on the screens, done again by the FM-1 itself. Press HOME + OP6 to open it. It holds 16 automations, and HOME + ARP + white key plays one from any screen; with REC before the white key it records one there.

The 16 slots

  • Each slot shows its number, the screen it was recorded on and its length in minutes and seconds; an empty one says EMPTY.
  • SELECT and ALGORITHM move between the slots, and a white key picks one: white key 1 is slot 00, white key 16 slot 0F. The keys play nothing here.

Recording

  • Press REC: AUTOMATIONS closes and you are back on the screen you came from. That screen is the automation's. A slot that already holds one asks first: REPLACE or KEEP.
  • From any screen, HOME + ARP + REC + white key records without opening AUTOMATIONS: with HOME's combination open (HOME tapped, or held), ARP switches its white keys to the automations, REC switches them from playing to recording (REC lights), and a white key records into its slot, white key 1 slot 00 to white key 16 slot 0F, on the screen you are on. Nothing needs holding, and none of these keys is recorded. A slot that already holds one asks first, as above; while an automation records or plays, none starts (ONE AUTOMATION AT A TIME).
  • The recording starts at your first press, key or turn, with no silence before it. Meanwhile the header shows the slot in red, AUTO 03, after a red dot.
  • Everything you do is recorded: keys, buttons and knob turns, HOME's combinations and every hold by its timing, and MIDI's learned controls as the controls they stand for. The MASTER knob is never recorded.
  • Press HOME + OP6 to end it, HOME held or tapped first: AUTOMATIONS opens again and says how long it is. Or press HOME + ARP + REC to end it where you are: you stay on your screen, and the FM-1 says how long it is (AUTOMATION 03 RECORDED: 0:42). The keys that end it are not recorded, and whatever you still hold is let go in the automation at that moment.
  • A recording is a change like any other: SAVE keeps it, AUTOMATIONS' REVERT drops it, and REVERT ALL ends it and drops it with your other changes. A minute of busy playing takes about 1 KB of the memory for edits; when that memory is full the recording stops there (MEMORY FOR EDITS IS FULL), keeping what it has.

Playing

  • Press PLAY: as you let it go, the FM-1 goes to the automation's screen and plays it back there, every press and turn at its time, as if made by hand: a fast knob turn takes the same bigger steps it took when you recorded it. The header shows the slot in green, AUTO 03, after a green play arrow.
  • From any screen, HOME + ARP + white key plays one without opening AUTOMATIONS: with HOME's combination open (HOME tapped, or held), ARP switches its white keys from the tracks' mutes to the automations, and a white key plays its slot as PLAY does, on the automation's own screen: white key 1 slot 00, white key 16 slot 0F. Nothing needs holding. ARP lights while the white keys are the automations, each one dim where its slot holds one, dark where it is empty; ARP again takes them back to the mutes.
  • A white key whose slot is empty says so (AUTOMATION 03 IS EMPTY), and the combination stays open for another key. An automation cannot start another: while one records or plays, the combination starts none (ONE AUTOMATION AT A TIME).
  • Nothing else is set first: the automation plays on whatever the FM-1 is doing. Have the right song, instrument or loops ready, as you had them when you recorded it.
  • Any key, button or knob of yours stops it (AUTOMATION STOPPED), and that press does nothing else. The MASTER knob stays yours throughout.
  • It plays once. At its end, what it holds is let go (AUTOMATION 03 PLAYED).
  • A SAVE in the automation saves, as yours does. While the save's progress shows, the FM-1 takes no input, so the playback waits: its time stands still, and it goes on from there once the save is over, with no step lost.
  • REVERT ALL stops it, and a page writing to the FM-1 (an upload, the EDITOR page's WRITE) stops a playback or ends a recording (AUTOMATION STOPPED).

Compacting: shorter pauses

Work done on the screens at a hand's pace (a few edits, a round of the menus) can play back as fast as the FM-1 takes it: COMPACT, in the menu, shortens an automation's pauses. Leave an automation of music as it is: its pauses are part of the music.

  • SEL, COMPACT opens a list of limits, COMPACT AUTOMATION 03: 1 S, 0.5 S and 0.3 S. Pick one with SELECT and press SEL: every pause while nothing is held that is longer than the limit is cut to it, and the FM-1 gives the new length (AUTOMATION 03 COMPACTED: 0:18). On an empty slot: AUTOMATION 03 IS EMPTY.
  • Only the pauses while nothing is held are shortened: from a step after which no key or button is down in the automation, to its next step. A pause while something is held keeps its time, as it is part of what that control does: a hold (SETTINGS' HOLD DELAY), the 2-second holds (FX held, EDIT held on LOOPS, HOME + REC held), a knob turned with SEL held, a note's length. Every step stays, in its order.
  • With no such pause longer than the limit: NOTHING TO COMPACT, and the slot is as it was.
  • Which limit: 0.3 s is the shortest, as it stays above COMBO GRACE's longest setting, 250 ms: a button let go within that time still makes a combination with the next press, so a shorter pause could join two separate presses. An automation whose steps wait for the music (a LOAD on DJ waiting for the bar line, notes on LOOPS waiting for QUANTIZE's line) wants a longer limit, so the music gets there first. Compacting again with a shorter limit goes further.
  • Compacting is a change like a recording: SAVE keeps it, AUTOMATIONS' REVERT drops it. With no room for it in the memory for edits: MEMORY FOR EDITS IS FULL, and the slot is as it was.

The menu

SEL opens the menu: RECORD, PLAY, COMPACT (above), CLEAR (it asks first) and, while there are changes not saved, REVERT, which brings back the automations as last saved.
